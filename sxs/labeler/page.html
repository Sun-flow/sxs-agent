Marked items

Drag on the screen to mark a button, text, icon or area.
Drag on the screen to draw a box. Click a box to select it, drag it to move it, drag its corner to resize it. Del removes the selected box, Ctrl+S saves, [ and ] go to the previous or next screen. Colours: blue button, green text, purple icon, red badge, orange region.
