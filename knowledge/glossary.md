# Glossary

<!-- index: Read when you meet a game word on screen or in a guide and need to know what it is and which page explains it -->

> **Summary:** 748 terms, one row each, grouped by first letter: what the word means in one or two lines and the page to read next. Spellings are the game's own; a source's misspelling is listed once under "Aliases and misspellings seen in sources". A ❓ marks a term or meaning known only from a tier D source or from the Chinese or Japanese version. Combat stats, with the maths the pages give, are collected under "Stats"; short forms under "Abbreviations and player slang".
> **Applies:** all seasons
> **Related:** [Conventions](CONVENTIONS.md), [Source register](sources/README.md), [Provenance](sources/provenance.md), every page in `systems/` and `classes/`; rules in `memory-bank/game/rules/` are cited by ID (for example R-120) where no knowledge page covers a term

How to read a row: **Kind** is one of currency, material, item, tool, system, mode, place, npc, class, skill, fantomon,
companion, stat, gear, relic, event, shop, ui, slang, source-alias. Status effects and Fantomon skills are filed
under skill; monsters, bosses and peoples under npc; gems, affixes and gear sets under gear. "Read more" is the best
page for the term; a bare rule ID means only our own rules describe it. Meanings carry no source tags: the linked
page has them. Where pages disagree, the row gives the best-supported meaning and says so.

## Stats

What each combat stat does. "Affix range" is the low to high roll of a single standard gear affix in the community
table labelled Champion III, Season 2 ([Gear](systems/gear.md#affixes)).

| Stat | What it does | Maths and notes |
|---|---|---|
| HP | Health pool. | Base stat. Affix: 12 to 18.7% or flat 17.1K to 26.8K. Max-HP shields (Gale Shield, Void Bubble) scale with it. The bot heals to 70% before a fight (R-061). |
| ATK | Attack. | Base stat. Every skill is a flat part ("Basic Damage") plus a percentage part that scales with ATK. Affix: 12 to 18.7% or flat 3.42K to 5.36K. |
| DEF | Defence. | Base stat. Affix: 12 to 18.7% or flat 3.42K to 5.36K. Knight shields and counters (Guardian Ring, Rebound) scale with it. |
| SPD | Speed. | Base stat; no page gives the turn-order formula ❓. Affix: 12 to 18.7% or flat 2.74K to 4.28K. The "SPD to ATK" affix converts 9.6 to 14.4% of it into ATK. |
| Crit Rate | Chance to crit. | A crit deals 150% base damage; Crit DMG adds to that ❓ (one tier D source, consistent with Fandom's "150%+"). Affix: 4.8 to 7.5%; dual line with Crit DMG 9.6 to 15.3%. Class stat of the Duelist line. |
| Crit DMG | Size of a crit above 150%. | Affix: 7.2 to 11.2%; dual line 14.4 to 23%. |
| Crit RES | Lowers the chance of being crit. | Also written Crit Res or Crit Resist. Affix: 4.8 to 7.5%. Tank companions and Tank Fantomons give it. |
| Accuracy | Lowers the target's Block chance. | 20% attacker Accuracy means the defender needs 120% Block to block everything ❓. Affix: 4.8 to 7.5%. Class stat of the Sorcerer line (a database site calls the trait Block Penetration). |
| Block Rate | Chance to block. | A blocked hit deals 50% and ignores the crit bonus ❓. Affix: 4.8 to 7.5%; dual line with Block Efficiency 9.6 to 15.3%. Class stat of the Knight line. Sages should not stack it. |
| Block Efficiency | How much a block cuts. | Dual line 14.4 to 23%. In Season 2 Knights prefer it over more Block Rate. No formula given ❓. |
| DMG Boost | More damage dealt, as a percentage. | Special stat from companions (level 20 and 70 ❓), Support Fantomon aptitudes, black gems (Obsidian) and Mythic relics. |
| DMG RES | Less damage taken, as a percentage. | Also "DMG Reduction". Dual affix with Healing Boost: 4.8 to 7.68%. White gems (Moonstone) carry it. |
| Healing Boost | Stronger heals. | Affix: 9.6 to 15%; dual line 19.2 to 30.7%. Orange gems (Amber) add 0.4% a tier. Companions give it only at level 100. |
| Effect Hit Rate | Makes your status effects land. | Modifies the "base chance" in skill text (Frozen 40%, Immobilize 30%, Erosion 50%). Flat affix 3.42K to 5.36K. The Mage charm Insight raises it. |
| Effect RES | Resists enemy status effects. | Flat affix 3.42K to 5.36K; "Effect RES to DEF" converts 7.5 to 11.2%. |
| Elemental Mastery | More elemental skill damage. | Flat affix 3.42K to 5.36K. Raised by Elemental Mystery and Elemental Harmony (one stack per element equipped, up to 4). |
| Elemental RES | Less elemental damage taken. | Flat affix 3.42K to 5.36K. |
| Physical Mastery | More Physical skill damage. | Flat affix 3.42K to 5.36K. |
| Physical RES | Less Physical damage taken. | Flat affix 3.42K to 5.36K. |
| PvE / PvP Bonus DMG and DMG RES | Damage dealt and taken in that kind of content. | Dual affix lines, 9.6 to 15.3% each. Paid Stellaris relics give PvP +4.8% ❓. |
| Affinity (element) | Damage-side elemental total from relics. | Shown per element at the Workbench; displayed relics count +20%; total affinity of one element opens a Gate of Elements (NPC companions lend 101 each). |
| Aegis (element) | Resist-side elemental total from relics. | Shown per element at the Workbench; grows with relic stars. |
| Element match-up | Which element hurts which. | Wind beats Water, Water beats Fire, Fire beats Wind. A monster halves damage from the element it beats, may be immune to it, and takes much more from the element that beats it. Physical is neutral. Light and Dark sit outside the triangle (Dark weak to Light per one blog ❓). |
| CP (Power) | One number for total strength. | Hard gate for dungeon difficulties, Gates of Power and tower floors. The bot fights up to 1.25x its own power (R-127). Most CP in a season comes from seasonal gear, which is scaled down to level 100 afterwards. |
| Skill level, star, quality | How a skill grows. | Level raises the flat part (Battle Essence). A star raises the flat part about 15%. A quality step raises flat and percentage parts: about 25% for Techniques, 20% for Charms. |
| Class damage range per hit | How swingy each Tier 2 class is. | Duelist 50 to 300%, Knight 50 to 200%, Sorcerer 100 to 250%, Sage 50 to 200%. |
| Fantomon share | How much of a Fantomon's stats you get. | Main 100%, each Sub 50%, the rest of the collection 20% ❓. |
| Relic Resonance | Bonus of displayed relics. | The four displayed relics of an element get +20% on elemental stats and effects. |
| 2x Rating Reward Rate | Chance to double dungeon rating rewards. | Region-specific gear line (+5% seen), Lucky Statue 10% (+2% when displayed), Astral Pact node +2%. They add up. |
| Gear Drop Quality Ascension Rate | Chance a dungeon drop comes one rarity higher. | Region-specific gear line (+10% seen); Astral Pact node +3%. |
| Season Rating Score | Season points that become Primostars. | Gear 38 points per average enhancement level; relic about 57, skill 13, character about 100, Fantomon 14 per seasonal level. Season 1: Primostars = 10 + score / 100. |
| Permanent dish value | Falling returns on stat food. | Dishes 1 to 10 of one kind give 100%, 11 to 30 give 50%, 31 to 50 give 20%, 51 and up give 5%. |
