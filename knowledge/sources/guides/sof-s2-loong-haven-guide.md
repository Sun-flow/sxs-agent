## Section I: Before S2 and pre-season (p. 4)

Final week of Season 1:
- Check that Primostars / Astral Pact are on track for your target. Targets: F2P 104 or 128 Primostars; light/mid spenders 152 or 176; stretch goal 200 [S-02].
- Rating: 2 Divine + 3 Mythic gear pieces from Hellhold give an SSS rating. SSS is also reachable with 2 Divine from Hellhold + 2 Divine from Aquaorigin + 1 Mythic from Hellhold (aggregate score) [S-02].
- Always run the latest dungeon if you can. If you only need gear for the rating, buy Aquaorigin Divine shards or whole pieces: they are (or soon will be) cheap [S-02].

Before entering Loong Haven:

| # | Do | Number |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stop collecting EXP from the bed, and turn off its "Auto Collect" | from 36 hours before launch |
| 2 | Bank stamina | 300 if possible |
| 3 | Stack the dungeon rest bonus for the new dungeon | 6 attempts if possible |

On entering Loong Haven (after claiming the stored bed EXP):
- Push the map for more statues first. (Or push and run the new dungeon to lock a ranking place, if you care) [S-02].
- Do NOT use the free daily speed-up or Temporal Crystals before the new Loong Haven statues are activated: with them active the same items give a much larger EXP boost [S-02].
- This is the PRE-SEASON of Season 2: the first dungeon does NOT count toward seasonal progression milestones [S-02].
- F2P can sell the shards/gear from the first dungeon for income: by the time the next dungeon unlocks, the cart should already drop Legendary gear stronger than this dungeon's Mythic gear [S-02].

## Section III: What's new (pp. 9-14)

### Affix Refinement, with Divinecraft Stones (p. 9)

- Affix Refinement lets you (partly) choose a gear piece's affixes; the guide calls it indispensable [S-02].
- **Divinecraft Stones** are the consumable. They come ONLY from dismantling gear [S-02].
- Spending stones re-rolls the affixes of a piece. Affixes can be locked so they are not re-rolled; each lock raises the cost [S-02]:

| Locked affixes | Divinecraft Stones per re-roll |
|---|---|
| 0 | 100 |
| 1 | 300 |
| 2 | 1200 |
| 3 | 3200 |

- Only Mythic or Divine gear can be refined. Rarity does NOT change the level of affix that can roll [S-02].
- **General rule:** 1-2 good affixes per piece is enough. More gets very expensive; 3-4 good affixes is whale territory. F2P and light spenders should put resources into other progression first [S-02].
- In this and ALL future seasons the first dungeon of a season is not "seasonal gear", so its affixes roll at their worst values. Gear from any later dungeon of the season gets a bonus on affixes: 200% rate for unique lines and 20% for standard lines [S-02].
- Affixes from first-dungeon gear CAN be transferred to seasonal gear, and the seasonal bonus then applies. So first-dungeon affixes are still useful [S-02].
- **Warning:** inheritance destroys the original piece. Check the direction: the good affixes must move onto the new piece, not the reverse [S-02].

Two schools of thought on the first (pre-season) dungeon (p. 10):

| View | Argument |
|---|---|
| 1. Do not push hard; sell | The gear is outclassed almost immediately (affix luck aside), so selling shards is guaranteed Dawnium |
| 2. Farm it for affixes | A good affix on Mythic gear has a very high value ceiling. Even on day 1 of the next dungeon you are unlikely to find 5 Legendary pieces to replace your Mythic set |

- Either way, being able to meet the next dungeon's CP threshold comes first [S-02].

### Affix Inheritance (p. 10)

| Kind | Rule |
|---|---|
| Season inheritance | Moving affixes from old gear to new gear is FREE, but the old piece is lost. Only like for like (sword to sword, shield to shield) |
| Cross-season inheritance | Starting in Season 2, all affixes can be inherited onto future seasons' gear |

- The author hunts for "top gear affixes" (own class and other classes) in the first dungeon, because they gain the 200% seasonal bonus once inherited onto the next dungeon's gear and can be carried forward [S-02].
- Watch the auction house for gear priced low by sellers who did not notice a valuable affix [S-02].

## Section IV: Starting Season 2, day 1 / week 1 (p. 15)

1. **Cart.** Start upgrading the cart with the resources stored from Season 1 (assuming the cart was maxed). The author still thinks Stone/Wood max is correct; Rolla max is viable [S-02].
2. **Material Realms.** If you saved tools from Season 1, WAIT. Returns scale with Kingdom rank, so it is more efficient to spend them at the maximum Kingdom rank; the low pressure of the pre-season and early season makes waiting safe [S-02].
   - Use only enough Rolla attempts to complete the daily quests. Keep all consumable Material Realm tools locked in the inventory until Champion III [S-02].
   - The maximum Kingdom rank in Loong Haven is **Champion III**, expected at **level 120** [S-02].
3. **T4 Ascension.** The Tier 4 class change unlocks at **level 106** [S-02].
4. **Relic pool.** The Loong Haven relic gacha has 2 pools. Pool 1 opens on day 1; Pool 2 opens about one month in (about 24 October). Pool #1 works as in Season 1: relics from the gacha AND from Destiny Fruits. Pool #2 is gacha ONLY [S-02].
5. **Demonbind Tower** opens at **level 116** (described as Loong Haven's Fantasia Ascent dungeon). It gives gems [S-02].

## Section V: Fantomons (pp. 16-17)

- Adult Fantomon evolution unlocks at **level 108**. It is a very large combat boost. It requires the Fantomon's skill at MYTHIC rarity, which for Nyxarchon / Aegiswing costs one duplicate from the exchange shop [S-02].
- **Pandarial** does not arrive until day 46 of the season (about 7 November): a month and a half of waiting [S-02].

What to do at season start:

| Situation | Do |
|---|---|
| Any | Do not wait 45 days for an Adult Fantomon; the lost progression is not worth it |
| You own Nyxarchon or Aegiswing | Buy one duplicate and make it Adult at once. Then stop spending and save all currency for Pandarial |
| You own the "wrong" Mythic for your class (e.g. DPS with Aegiswing) | Do NOT buy Nyxarchon from scratch now. Make the Aegiswing you have (or an Epic filler) Adult for the CP, and save the rest for Pandarial |
| You own neither | Make a filler Fantomon Adult (e.g. Armopi or Zeioletus) and save all remaining currency to make Pandarial Adult the moment it releases |

Long-term roadmap (late Season 2 and beyond) (p. 17):
- Pandarial's active skill is extremely strong, so it becomes the primary **Deployed** Fantomon. Nyxarchon and Aegiswing are valued for their **aptitudes** (Crit/Accuracy and Block) [S-02].

| Order | DPS classes | Tanks / healers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mythic Nyxarchon: make Adult first for the immediate spike | Mythic Aegiswing: make Adult first |
| 2 | Immortal Pandarial: push its skill to Immortal (main Deployed unit) | Immortal Pandarial: push to Immortal for its Deployed active skill |
| 3 | Immortal Nyxarchon: maximises aptitudes; an Immortal Nyxarchon gives roughly 30% Crit Rate and Accuracy, freeing gear affix slots | Immortal Aegiswing: maximise its aptitudes |
| 4 | Mythic Aegiswing: last, mainly for PvP utility (can be made Adult after the other two if needed for PvP; Pandarial is much better in 4v4) | - |
| Aptitude focus | All resources into Nyxarchon (Crit Rate / Accuracy) | Tank: all into Aegiswing (Block / Block Efficiency). Support: all into Pandarial (Healing Boost) |

## Section VI: Progression strategy (pp. 18-24)

### Permanent vs seasonal investment (p. 18)
- From Season 2 on, permanent power becomes LESS important from mid-season and seasonal power MORE important, because dungeons and progression are harder than in Season 1. Over-investing in permanent power risks being unable to clear late-season content [S-02].
- For ALL seasons [S-02]:

| Season phase | Stamina and Material Realms skew toward | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Beginning | Sand | Boosting relics gives the most power early |
| Middle | Ore | Gear catches up with relics |

- Pulling relics early in a season is a big power source: the pool is new, so each pull is worth more than in a saturated pool [S-02].

### Material Realm (p. 18)
- Recommended split is still 4444 or 4244 (depending on Rolla needs). 6446 is fine if you are desperate for power. Material Realms stay a top priority [S-02].

### Dawnium (p. 19)
- Daily spending is unchanged from the first guide (same infographic, image only):

| Priority | Line | What exactly | Dawnium |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Daily Dungeon | Refresh x2 | 250 |
| 2 | Material Realms | 4 / 2 / 4 / 4 extra tools | 1080 |

| | **Total daily** | | **2000** |

- Early-season Dawnium beyond that: into the relic gacha. As the gacha saturates and mid-season nears, shift to heavy Material Realm spending [S-02].
- There are 2 relic gachas in Season 2. Ideally have tickets saved for the second one when it opens mid-season, because by then Dawnium is better spent on seasonal content. The second pool need not be rushed; fill it slowly through the season [S-02].

### Gems (p. 20)
- Gems are a good but not great form of progression: front-loaded. Season 2 adds more ways to get them: found in the open world (like ingredients) and from the Demonbind Tower from level 116 [S-02].
- Early they are a significant power source; later they are a very small share of total stats. Do not invest heavily unless you want an immediate, somewhat temporary boost [S-02].
- Season 2 raises gem slots per gear piece from 1 to 2: 10 gems in total [S-02].
- Expect gem prices to crash as players saturate and realise gems are a small part of power. Wait for that crash before buying higher-tier gems from the market [S-02].
- Combining beyond tier 5 can fail. Each failure fills a pity meter that eventually reaches a 100% success rate; a success resets the pity [S-02].

Gem upgrade trick [S-02]:

| Step | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Know that same-tier gems share one success rate (and pity) |
| 2 | Combine cheap gems of that tier (e.g. unwanted T5 gems) to build pity |
| 3 | At 100% pity, combine the expensive gems for a guaranteed success |
