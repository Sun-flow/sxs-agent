## Introduction (p. 3)

- The stated purpose: help players understand the game, avoid common mistakes and get stronger [S-01].
- Image only: a ranking screenshot shows power values of about 926K-942K at ranks 92-100 on the author's server at the time of writing (around 1 September 2026) [S-01]. Names omitted.

## Progression philosophy (pp. 4-5)

- Do not measure a F2P account against a whale's account [S-01].
- The guide was written mid-season, so it leans toward permanent power "for now" and plans a more seasonal focus from the start of the next season [S-01].
- Staying on schedule matters most: know when new content (especially new dungeons) unlocks and what it requires. If a threshold can realistically be reached on time, reach it first, then move resources back to permanent progression [S-01].
- A carry can clear a dungeon for you but cannot build or rank your account. Spend your own resources on things nobody can carry [S-01].
- You do not need to be strong enough to clear every group dungeon alone: you only need to meet the entry requirement ("get through the door"), then stronger players can do the clear [S-01].
- This is where +drop / +dungeon reward gear becomes very valuable (detailed on p. 8) [S-01].
- Avoid the cycle "build seasonal power, season resets, rebuild": seasonal power stacked on a strong permanent base is worth more [S-01].
- The guide is general advice, not an account audit; there is not always one correct answer [S-01].

## 2. Exploration / Kingdom Progression (pp. 7-9)

Chain stated by the guide: faster map progress -> more Lost Goddess Statues unlocked -> more passive EXP -> stronger character [S-01] (p. 7).

Three ways to progress the map (p. 7):

1. **Combat power.** More power clears more areas and activates more statues [S-01].
2. **Kingdom tools.** They open extra areas, and such an area always holds at least one more statue. Buying them from the kingdom shops is the top priority [S-01].

| Kingdom | Tool |
|---|---|
| Verdantglade | Withering Potion |
| Cinder Ridge | Magic Drill |
| Aqualis | Water Mine |
| All kingdoms | Gateway Key |

3. **Daily Commissions.** They give no direct power but are called invaluable. Complete them aggressively, even when they cost stamina. Reward priority: Premium Commissions (chance for recipes to sell, or journals that unlock new Companions) and Kingdom Currency (to buy more kingdom tools) [S-01].

Image only (p. 7):
- Item card "Mysterious Merchant's Journal": type Gift, source Commission; text says it is a gift to get acquainted with the Mysterious Merchant and, after that, raises Affinity. The caption says journals from premium commissions go toward unlocking new Companions [S-01].
- Item card "Abundance Leaf": type Currency (the Verdantglade kingdom currency; the author owned 1.86K) [S-01].
- Two commission cards: "Eliminate Dark Slimes" (0/3; rewards 150 Abundance Leaf, 11.5K of a gold-coloured coin, 8 of a sack item) and "Collect Vital Thornballs" (0/1, marked Combinable; rewards 150 of a gear-shaped currency, 2 and 5 of two other items). Each card also shows a heart with 50 and "+120" ❓ (meaning not explained) [S-01].
- Caption: a key/potion in Verdantglade costs 3k leaves, so the author pushes commissions that pay leaves until the tool is affordable; same for other kingdoms' currencies [S-01].

### Combat power and +drop gear (p. 8)

- Pushing dungeon difficulties for higher-rarity gear is one of the fastest power sources, but difficulties are gated by both character level and total combat power [S-01].
- **Rule:** keep any gear that has one of the stats below, as long as you can still meet the CP threshold of the newest dungeon while wearing it [S-01].

| Where (image only) | Exact stat text | Value shown |
|---|---|---|
| Legendary boots "Ocean Greaves +95" (Level 80, 39.3K) | "Aqualis Dungeons - 2x Rating Reward Rate" | +5% |
| Epic boots "Tidechaser's Leggings +95" (Level 87, 35.7K) | "Aqualis Dungeons - Gear Drop Quality Ascension Rate" | +10% |
| Relic "Lucky Statue" (Grand Treasure Hunt relic; Light Affinity 200; shown at 0 shards) | "Dungeon: 2 x Rating Reward Rate" | 10%, with a green "(+2%)" note beside it |

- Other stats visible on the two example boots (not highlighted): SPD 6.61K / 2.64K, Elemental Mastery 483 and 567, SPD 8.54%; and SPD 6.04K, ATK 3.02K, Elemental Mastery 549, Healing Boost 7.32% [S-01].
- How to use it: equip the +drop set before entering the highest dungeon you can enter, and ask for a carry. It raises the chance of a doubled shard/gear reward and the chance that a drop is upgraded in rarity (a Legendary drop can become Mythic) [S-01].
- Loop: faster dungeon gear -> higher CP -> next difficulty -> repeat. It also helps Fantasia Ascent floors and map exploration [S-01].
- After gearing is finished, keep using the set: extra shards can be sold for Dawnium and reinvested [S-01].
- Note 1: the gear stat is region specific. Aqualis-dungeon stats only work in Aqualis dungeons, and so on. Wear the matching one [S-01].
- Note 2: always keep the Lucky Statue in the main (displayed) relic line for the 20% resonance bonus [S-01].
- ❓ The meaning of the "(+2%)" annotation is not stated (it may be the 20% display bonus applied to 10%).

## 3. Permanent vs Seasonal Power (p. 10)

| Kind | Systems |
|---|---|
| Permanent | Relics, Fantomon, Companions, Astral Pact |
| Seasonal | Skills, Gear, Relic Resonance ("sort of": see Relics) |

- Permanent power stays forever; seasonal power is erased when you enter a new season [S-01].
- From Season 2 on, permanent power matters less by mid-season and seasonal power matters more for reaching higher dungeon modes [S-01].

End-of-Season-1 plan (written with about 3.5 weeks left):

1. Put seasonal power first only as far as needed to meet minimum dungeon thresholds; otherwise invest in permanent progression before Season 2 [S-01].
2. Focus on Ore to raise gear levels, because gear level weighs heavily in the end-of-season Astral Pact rating [S-01].
3. Invest in the Relic Gacha, ideally to 70% completion in each region [S-01].
4. Stop pulling skills; save pull resources for T4 [S-01].
5. Keep building Fantomons and Companions [S-01].

- Because of the end-of-season rating, seasonal progression cannot be dropped completely. The +drop strategy gears you faster, which frees resources for permanent progression. Upgrade gear levels at all points in the season [S-01].

Stamina / Material Realm rule (repeated on p. 15):

| Season phase | Put stamina and extra Material Realm runs into | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Beginning | Sand | Relic Resonance gives the most seasonal power early |
| Middle | Ore | Gear catches up with relic power |

## Relics (p. 11)

- Priority: produce as much Chrono Sand as reasonably possible and level Relic Resonance in ALL five elements. Do not level one element and ignore the others [S-01].
- Soft resonance cap: +10 in Season 1, +13 in Season 2, rising as the seasonal level cap rises. Levels above the cap reset at season end [S-01].
- Relic Resonance is a major source of both seasonal and permanent power [S-01].

Resonance priority (which relics to put in the displayed line and level first):

| Rank | Relic kind | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unique relic | Lucky Statue, Primal Gem |
| 2 | Special stats (class dependent) | Crit Rate, Block % |
| 3 | Fixed value | ATK 100 |
| 4 | Percent value | ATK 10% |

- Image only: the author's Light relic line at "Resonance Level: 10", four slots each at +10, labelled Flat Atk / Dungeon Bonus Drop (the Lucky Statue) / Flat Atk / Crit Dmg. Screen text: elemental stats and effects of relics on display increase by 20% [S-01].

Element to max first, per class (level the others afterwards):

| Class | Element | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Duelist | Fire | |
| Sage | Dark | |
| Knight | Water or Light | Water for AOE; Light otherwise |
| Sorcerer | Wind, Water, Fire or Light (preference) | Wind: PvP. Water: better in 4v4 than PvE. Fire: better in PvE than PvP. Light: equally good in both |

- Aim for 70% overall completion per region, with as many rare/epic relics as possible, before moving on [S-01].
- Advanced: each kingdom's Mythic/Legendary relics focus on different special stats; this matters only for later optimisation [S-01].

## Fantomons (p. 12)

- **Main is not Deployed.** The Deployed Fantomon fights beside you, so its passive ability matters more than its aptitudes. The Main Fantomon is chosen for its aptitudes, not its ability. Example: Nyxarchon as Main, Aegiswing as Deployed [S-01].
- Full lineup: one Fantomon of each type in the Sub role, plus the Main [S-01].
- Aptitude investment: max the Main first. Do not upgrade the aptitudes of the Sub that shares the Main's attribute; invest only in the Main and the two Subs of different attributes [S-01].
- Fantomon aptitude materials and summon tokens are the highest-priority Guild shop purchases every week [S-01].

## Companions (p. 13)

- New Companions are unlocked with journals from Premium Commissions [S-01].
- Each Companion gives special stats once levelled to a certain point and flat general stats otherwise. Favour the ones with the special stats your class needs, but follow the level order strictly. Companions give a large amount of stats [S-01].

Level order (the guide says not to deviate):

| Step | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Every Companion to level 20 |
| 2 | Class-matching Companions to level 50 |
| 3 | Every Companion to level 50 |
| 4 | Class-matching Companions to level 70 |
| 5 | Repeat the pattern until level 100 |

- Each Companion has favoured gifts; use only those on that Companion [S-01].

Stat priority (at Companion level 50):

| Role | Stats |
|---|---|
| Knight | Block / Crit Resist |
| DPS | Crit Rate / Accuracy |
| Healer | Preference; Healing Boost only unlocks at Companion level 100 |

## 4. Resource Economy

### Dawnium (p. 14)

- Goal "stronger this season": spend F2P Dawnium on extra Material Realm tools above the recommended daily amount (more skill materials, gear ore, gold, relic sand -> CP and unlocks) [S-01].
- Goal "long-term account strength, slower now": the relic gacha is generally the better investment. A balance of both is needed [S-01].
- The daily target below is in priority order. Aim for the full 2,000 Dawnium per day. When short, buy from the top and cut the optional lines first: the Gold Tool and the variable Vault purchases [S-01].

Image only: "Dawnium Daily Spend" infographic.

| Priority | Line | What exactly | Dawnium |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Daily Dungeon | Refresh x2 (maximise dungeon attempts every day) | 250 |
| 2 | Material Realms | Extra tools in the split 4 / 2 / 4 / 4 (Ore / Rolla / Battle Essence / Sand, per p. 15) | 1080 |
| 3 | Guild Donations | Donate only 3 times (70 Dawnium) + Gold Tool (200 Dawnium), "for best output of guild coins" | 270 |
| 4 | Vault | 400 Dawnium of purchases (valuable items and limited resources; variable) | 400 |
| | **Total daily** | | **2000** |

- ❓ The per-tool price behind the 1080 is not broken down, nor is the cost of each of the two dungeon refreshes or each donation.

### Material Realms (p. 15)

- "4244" and "4444" are the numbers of bonus Material Realm tools bought per day [S-01].
- F2P suggestion: 4 Ore, 2 Rolla, 4 Battle Essence, 4 Sand. Adjust to the account [S-01].
- The choice depends on the cart's Rolla level: if gold (Rolla) was upgraded as the 3rd or 4th cart priority after Wood/Stone, less Dawnium is needed for the Rolla realm. 4444 is the most popular; the author prefers 4244 [S-01].
- The Material Realm is called extremely important for seasonal power and must not be neglected [S-01].
- Advanced strategies exist but are not covered (except the saving tip on p. 23) [S-01].
- The Sand-early / Ore-mid rule from p. 10 is repeated [S-01].

### Cart (pp. 16-17)

Upgrade order (two accepted variants):

| Variant | Order |
|---|---|
| A (author's) | Wood and Stone (Stone maxed first, as the more demanding resource) -> Rolla -> Ore / Sand -> Battle Essence -> Treats |
| B | Wood and Stone -> Ore / Sand -> Rolla -> Battle Essence -> Treats |

Wood and Stone first (the popular strategy):

| | Points |
|---|---|
| Objective | Maximise cart efficiency and long-term average income |
| Downsides | Wood and Stone give no direct power; early-season power is lower, especially in Season 1 |
| Advantages | The cart levels faster; storage overflow is reached earlier; next season the cart starts at a higher level than other players' carts (trade first-season gains for higher average income in all later seasons; this is about overall average returns, not one resource) |
| Requirement | Be consistent every season. Skipping even one season likely loses most of the advantage and may force a restart |

Rolla first (the alternative, p. 17):

- Idea: produce as much Rolla as possible (extra Rolla runs in the Material Realm, maxing the cart's Rolla, even stamina) to refresh the Vendor Booth more often and buy Wood and Stone there [S-01].
- Why some use it: the Vendor Booth also sells Stellatie, bond trinkets and other items, so more refreshes add up to a lot of extras [S-01].
- Why it is less popular: Wood and Stone per refresh are limited, and the overall cart level ends up lower [S-01].
- The author calls it valid but not worthwhile: it needs careful balancing and more effort [S-01].
- Cart level also affects gear: a higher cart level gives a higher chance to roll high-tier Legendary gear, for free, all season. Example: when Aquaorigin opened, the author's cart dropped a Legendary piece 15k CP above their current Mythic piece, and two cart pieces were swapped in before entering the dungeon [S-01].

## 5. Gems (p. 18)

- At the time of writing the gem system was a few days from release [S-01].
- Gems are slotted into gear for extra stats. They are a good permanent power source at first but do not scale well over a season: no need to invest heavily [S-01].
- 1 socket per gear piece; 1 more socket per gear piece with each new season [S-01].
- Eight colours: red, purple, green, blue, orange, yellow, black, white [S-01].

## 6. Astral Pact & Primostars (pp. 19-20)

- The Astral Pact is an end-of-season rating system that grants bonuses by the number of Primostars awarded. 1 Primostar = 1 level on the track [S-01].
- Primostars come from seasonal progression in five rated categories: season gear level, season character level, season Fantomon level, season skill level, season relic level [S-01].

Image only (p. 19, left): Astral Pact reward track (first 25 levels; the screen has two tabs, "Astral Destiny" and "Astral Pact", and a "Hold to Fill" button):

| Astral Pact level | Bonus |
|---|---|
| 5 | ATK +1% |
| 10 | [Dungeon] Gear Drop Ascension Rate +3% |
| 15 | DEF +1% |
| 20 | Gem Drop Rate +5% |
| 25 | HP +1% |
| above 25 | ❓ cut off in the screenshot |

Image only (p. 19, right): the author's Season 1 rating screen, titled "S1. Witching Hours", tab Progression, 4d 17h before season end:

| Category | Level shown | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Season Gear Level | 133 | 5054 |
| Season Relic Level | 53 | 3021 |
| Season Skill Level | 176 | 2288 |
| Season Character Level | 22 | 2232 |
| Season Fantomon Level | 69 | 966 |
| **Progression Rating (total)** | SSS | **13561** (bar shows 13561/99999999) |
| Converts to | | **145 Primostars** |

- Screen text: season levels reset after the season ends and convert to Primostar; the reward is claimable when the season ends [S-01].
- Gear is weighted far more than the other categories (5054 of 13561 points in the example). To maximise Primostars, especially near season end with limited resources, upgrade gear as much as possible [S-01] (p. 20).
- Reference target for F2P: at least 104 Primostars in Season 1; a reference point, not a must [S-01].
- The Astral Pact is permanent progression that scales with seasonal progression: bonuses accumulate across all seasons and stay active [S-01].
- The author calls it the MOST important permanent progression, because it is computed once per season (relics can still be pulled later), but says not to plan the whole season around it [S-01].
- ❓ The score needed per Primostar and for each rating letter is not given (only the one data point: 13561 points = SSS = 145 Primostars).

## 7. Additional Tips (pp. 21-23)

### 7.1 Grand Treasure Hunt (p. 21)
- Every other week, save the event currency so that you can afford the Lucky Statue and Primal Gem relics [S-01]. The calendar on p. 6 shows which weeks are SAVE and which are MUST BUY.

### 7.2 Molly's Deals (p. 21)
- Check the gate at home through the week. It opens the friends list; an orange house symbol next to a friend means a special Molly's Deal is available there [S-01].
- Up to 10 deals can be bought per week; they are usually very good. She sells Stellatie, Covenite, relic gacha badges and similar items at a discount [S-01].
- Image only: the wooden gate with a bell; the friends list icons (a yellow "visit home" icon and the orange house icon); the Molly's Deals screen showing "Weekly Purchase Limit: 2/10" and one purchased offer: an item x10 with a 50%-off tag, priced 225 (Dawnium icon) [S-01].

### 7.3 Arena (p. 21)
- Save Arena tickets toward the end of each week. Right after the weekly reset, climb as high as possible before stronger players start [S-01].
- Even if the rank cannot be held, time spent at a higher rank gives a head start in weekly Arena points [S-01].
- At the start of the week the ladder is full of bots; with enough saved or bought tickets the #1 spot can be taken immediately [S-01].

### 7.4 Skill Vouchers (p. 22)
- Called one of the most important items. Most skills become obsolete at a new class tier, but some techniques or charms persist across seasons; if such a skill is core to the kit, spend Skill Vouchers to max its rank [S-01].
- Duelist-line example: at T4 (class "Conqueror") the charm "Piercing Assault" is the skill to put all vouchers into; most players think it is worth maxing even if you switch class at T5 [S-01].
- ❓ No equivalent skill is named for the Mage line or other classes.

### 7.5 Chaos Invasion (p. 22)
- After the event ends, the Mysterious Merchant appears in your home and sells items for the event currency [S-01].
- Buy ONLY the Primal Forge. Keep buying it until it is maxed. Skip everything else [S-01].
- If you cannot afford it, send the merchant away and save the currency for the next Chaos Invasion [S-01].
- Damage tips: the boss has very low armor, which favours burst damage and team buffs. Enter as a party even though it can be soloed [S-01].
- Best setup per run: 1 damage dealer + 3 supports, for all classes including tanks and supports. Run 4 rounds, each player taking one turn as the damage dealer, to maximise each player's score [S-01].
- Most players are DPS, so such parties are hard to form; it is optional but helps ranking [S-01].

### 7.6 Wheel of Destiny (p. 23)

| Priority | Options to pick |
|---|---|
| 1 (top) | Skill Shard Vouchers |
| 2 | Gateway keys; auroral elemental badges |
| 3 | Legendary auroral badges; Covenite; bond trinkets |
| 4 | Legendary Fantomon resources (wool, egg, essence); 2888 Dawnium; additional lucky tickets |

### 7.7 Material Realms: saving uses (p. 23)
- Community strategy; the author cannot guarantee it is better [S-01].
- Material Realm rewards scale with Kingdom rank. So you can save unused attempts and spend them after reaching the maximum Kingdom rank for better returns [S-01].
- While saving, run 10 Rolla per day to still complete daily quests (written as 0/10/0/0) [S-01].
- From Season 2 every kingdom has only three ranks, so waiting for the top rank is shorter [S-01].
- It is an optimisation, not a requirement: never hoard attempts if that stops you from reaching important CP or progression thresholds [S-01].
