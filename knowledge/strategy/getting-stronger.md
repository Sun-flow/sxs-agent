# Getting stronger: what to do at a power wall

<!-- index: Read when a zone, gate or dungeon needs more power than the account has: the order in which to raise power, cheapest first -->

> **Summary:** work down the list; stop as soon as the wall is passed. Free and permanent steps come first,
> resource-costing seasonal steps later, Dawnium last. If the list is exhausted, the wall is a time wall: collect
> idle output and come back.
> **Applies:** all seasons
> **Related:** [Resource priorities](resource-priorities.md), [Daily routine](daily-routine.md),
> `docs/step5-upgrades.md`, rules R-080, R-118

## Which stat a wall needs

- Zone and gate cards compare against total power: any step above helps; a Gate of Elements compares total
  elemental affinity, so relics are the answer [R-086].
- Dungeon thresholds are entry checks on power; the clear itself can be carried [S-01].
- A lost fight at equal power is usually an element or loadout problem for a Mage: swap the loadout before
  spending resources [S-03].
