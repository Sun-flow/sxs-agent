# Season playbook

<!-- index: Read when a season is ending, starting, or to check what the current season phase asks for: checklists for the change-over and priorities for early, middle and late season -->

> **Summary:** a season is one kingdom, a level band above 100, a row of dungeons opening on fixed days, and a
> rating at the end that buys permanent Astral Pact levels. Bank resources into the new season, light the new
> statues before using speed-ups, put the first weeks into relics and rank, the middle into gear and dungeon
> thresholds, and the end into gear level.
> **Applies:** every season from the first change-over on. Season 1 is days 1 to 46 on the guide's server,
> Season 2 days 47 to 113 [S-02].
> **Related:** [Seasons](../systems/seasons.md), [Astral Pact](../systems/astral-pact.md), [Dungeons](../systems/dungeons.md),
> [Progression roadmap](progression-roadmap.md), data in [season-schedule.json](../data/season-schedule.json)

## What resets and what stays

| Resets at season end | Stays |
|---|---|
| Character level above 100 | Relic collection and relic ascensions |
| Skill levels above 100 | Skill ascensions (stars and quality) |
| Fantomon levels above 100 | Fantomon ascensions and aptitudes |
| Gear enhancement above +100; season gear is scaled down to level 100 | Companion levels |
| Relic resonance above the season cap (+10 in Season 1, +13 in Season 2) | Guild buffs, permanent dish buffs |
| | Cart level; Astral Pact levels |
| | Gear affixes, by inheritance (from Season 2) |

Sources: [S-03][S-01][S-02]. Whether wallet currencies and stored tools carry over is not stated; guides that tell
players to save tools and shards across the change imply tools do ❓.

## Last two weeks of a season

1. Check the rating: Character screen, Astral Pact. Targets: Season 1, 104 or 128 Primostars for a free player;
   Season 2, 680 then 800 [S-01][S-02].
2. All ore into gear enhancement: gear is the heaviest of the five rating parts [S-01]. One Primostar is about
   2.6 gear levels in Season 1 [S-51]. See "What the rating counts" below.
3. Gear rating: the newest dungeon's pieces count most (Season 1 SSS = 2 Divine + 3 Mythic Hellhold, or 2 Divine
   Hellhold + 2 Divine Aquorigin + 1 Mythic Hellhold). Cheap shards of the previous dungeon can fill gaps [S-02].
4. Stop pulling skills; save Stellatie, Covenite and vouchers for the next class tier [S-01].
5. Sell spare shards and gear before the rollover [S-53].
6. Buy area tools for the new kingdom's map if kingdom currency allows: eog suggests 5 Gateway Keys, 2 Water
   Mines, 1 Magic Drill, 1 Withering Potion [S-53]; its map data shows Loong Haven needs exactly 5 keys, 2 mines,
   1 drill, 1 potion and 7 Cloud Jade, the new kingdom's own item [S-85].

## What the rating counts

eog's star calculator [S-85] (tier C; data: [astral-pact-scoring.json](../data/astral-pact-scoring.json)). Points
count only levels above the season floor (Lv 100 and relic +10 in Season 1; Lv 130 and +13 in Season 2).

| Per level above the floor | Season 1 | Season 2 | Season 3 | Counted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Character level | 100 | 100 | 100 | 1 |
| Gear enhancement, per piece | 38 | 18 | 14 | 5 pieces |
| Skill level, per slot | 13 | 7 | 5 | 8 slots |
| Relic resonance, per slot | 57 | 33 | 26 | 20 slots |
| Fantomon level, per slot | 14 | 8 | 6 | 4 slots |
| Points per Primostar | 100 | 27 | 13 | |
| Flat Primostars | 10 | 45 | 65 | |

- One level on all five gear pieces is 190 points in Season 1, close to 2 Primostars; gear has by far the most
  levels to gain, which is why SOF calls it the heaviest part [S-01].
- One relic resonance level on all 20 displayed relics is 1,140 points, about 11 Primostars, but only three such
  levels exist above the floor (+11 to +13) and they reset. Worth doing late in a season when sand is spare; SOF's
  order (sand early for power, ore late for rating) is unchanged.
- Season 1 milestones [S-85]: 10, 20, 30, 40, 56, 72, 88, 104, 128, 152, 176, 200, 230, 260, 290, 320 Primostars,
  cycling Ascension drop rate +3%, Gem acquisition +5%, Dungeon double reward +2%, EXP gain +2%. The free-player
  targets land on EXP gain +2% (104) and Ascension drop rate +3% (128).

## Last 36 hours

| Do | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Stop collecting bed EXP; turn Auto Collect off | The stored EXP is claimed in the new season (idle storage holds 36 hours [S-87]) | [S-02][S-85] |
| Let stamina fill to 300 | Spend it on the new kingdom's sand | [S-02] |
| Skip Daily Dungeon runs to bank 6 | Six runs of the new dungeon on day one | [S-02] |
| Keep the free speed-up and Temporal Crystals | They pay more after the new statues | [S-02] |
| Keep Material Realm tools | Rewards scale with the new kingdom's rank | [S-02] |

## Launch day

1. Claim the stored bed EXP and season rewards; spend all Primostars in the Astral Pact that morning [S-85].
2. Push the new kingdom's map for statues. No speed-ups until the reachable statues are lit [S-02].
3. Then the free speed-up and Temporal Crystals. eog's milestones for speed-ups: Lv 106 (Tier 4), Lv 108 (Adult
   Fantomon), Lv 116 (tower, gems), Lv 130 (second dungeon); or hold them to the season's end [S-85].
4. First dungeon of the season: it is pre-season. Its gear does not count toward the season's milestones and its
   affixes roll without the seasonal bonus, though good affixes can be inherited later. Two valid lines: sell the
   shards for sure income, or keep rolling for affixes [S-02].
5. Relic pool 1: spend the early Dawnium here [S-02].

## Middle (each new dungeon)

- A dungeon opens every 13 days in Season 2. Meet the threshold on the day, bank 6 runs, get carried on the
  highest difficulty you can enter [S-02][S-01].
- Stamina and tools switch from sand to ore [S-01].
- Realm tools 4244 or 4444 daily; 6446 only when a threshold is at risk [S-02].
- Affixes: 1 or 2 good lines per piece are enough; refining further is too expensive for a free player. Check the
  direction before inheriting: the source piece is destroyed [S-02]. See [Gear](../systems/gear.md).
- Gems: do not invest; wait for market prices to fall [S-02].
- Relic pool 2 (about a month in): use saved tickets, fill slowly [S-02].
