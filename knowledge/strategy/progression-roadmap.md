# Progression roadmap: from a new account through every season

<!-- index: Read to see which phase the account is in, what the goals and exit conditions of that phase are, and what comes next -->

> **Summary:** nine phases. Each has a goal, the things to do in order, and an exit condition. Phases 1 to 4 are
> Season 1 (three kingdoms, level 1 to about 124), phase 5 is the first season change, phases 6 to 8 repeat every
> season after that, and phase 9 is the forecast from the Chinese version.
> **Applies:** all seasons. Dates in the sources are for the guide author's server (eog counts from the global
> launch, day 1 = 2026-05-19 [S-85]); our server's day 1 is not known yet ❓, so this page uses server days and
> levels, never dates.
> **Related:** [Strategy index](README.md), [Seasons](../systems/seasons.md), [Levels and unlocks](../systems/levels-and-unlocks.md),
> [Dungeons](../systems/dungeons.md), [Season playbook](season-playbook.md), [Mage path](mage-path.md)

## The road at a glance

| Phase | Where | Level | Server day (guide's server) | Main gates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Originisle | 1 to about 13 | 1 | Tutorial, first class (Warrior or Mage) |
| 1 | Verdantglade | to 40 | 1 to 4 | Rank Apprentice I to III, Auroradrasil dungeon (Hard 36K) |
| 2 | Cinder Ridge | 40 to about 82 | 4 to 12 | Arena 40, second class 44, Fantomons 50 (51 by R-011), Mecha Summit (Hard 180K) |
| 3 | Aqualis | about 82 to 100 | 12 to 24 | Third class (Lv 82), Ocean Palace Ruins (Hard 580K) |
| 4 | Season 1 end game | 100 to about 124 | 24 to 46 | Aquorigin Shrine (930K), Hellhold (2.25M), Astral Pact rating |
| 5 | Season change | 100 | 46 to 47 | Bank bed EXP, stamina, dungeon runs |
| 6 | New season, early | 100 to 120 | first 2 to 3 weeks | New statues, new relic pool, top rank, new class tier |
| 7 | New season, middle | 120+ | dungeons 2 to 4 | Each dungeon threshold on its day; ore and gear |
| 8 | New season, late | cap | last dungeon to the end | Gear level for Primostars |
| 9 | Later seasons | | | Same loop, new kingdom and class tier each time |

Thresholds and days: [Dungeons](../systems/dungeons.md) [S-01][S-02][S-03]. An account that starts late on an old
server is behind the calendar: the dungeons are already open, so their thresholds are simply the next walls to
climb, in order ❓ (our reading; no source discusses late starts).

## Phase 0: Originisle (tutorial)

- Follow the quest tracker; every fight is automatic [R-044].
- At the Guardian Colossi the class is chosen: **Mage** for this account (mae's decision) [R-054].
- Exit: the Voyage to Verdantglade.

## Phase 1: Verdantglade (level 1 to 40)

Goal: light every statue you can reach, hit each level cap with the rank materials ready, enter the first dungeon.

1. Follow the main quest zone by zone; clear each zone to 100% and bless its statue before leaving [R-070].
2. Side zones (Lost Goddess Statues) whenever their recommended power is within reach (our 1.25x rule) [R-065].
3. Advance rank at Lv 20 and 30; pick Bellflowers a few levels early [R-118].
4. Gear: wear the best piece, enhance all pieces evenly in bands of five levels [R-092]; skills by Quick
   Upgrade [R-104]; fill every charm slot [R-080].
5. Cart: raise every row to Lv 10 (the publisher's tip [S-87]), then Stone and Wood first [S-01][R-136].
   Dismantle spare gear from Lv 12 at the Alchemy Pot [S-87].
6. Join a guild at Lv 15 to 20 (Lv 15 officially [S-87], Lv 20 on our account [R-011]): donate daily, start AFK gathering, do Conquest [R-102][R-103][R-112].
7. Save Abundance Leaves for a Withering Potion (opens Stagnant Jungle) and a Gateway Key (Rabbit Vault): each
   hides a statue [S-01]. See [Shops](../systems/shops.md). The whole Verdantglade map needs 3 Gateway Keys
   (Rabbit Vault, Gorge Depths, Owl Vault), 5 Withering Potions (Jungle Vault, Monolith Ruins V, Stagnant Jungle,
   Sunken Forest, Sunrise Isle) and 3 Magic Drills (Vine Peninsula, Hidden Camp, Hideout) [S-85].
8. Daily Dungeon Auroradrasil as soon as power passes its threshold (Hard 36K): two runs a day, with NPC
   companions if no players [S-03].
9. Start the Prayer habit: x10 pulls only, keep the 200-prayer pick for a core skill [R-093]. See [Mage path](mage-path.md).

Exit: Lv 40, Apprentice III done, the next rank ready, the Voyage Obelisk to Cinder Ridge open.

## Phase 2: Cinder Ridge (level 40 to about 82)

Goal: the second class, Fantomons, Arena and the market running; keep the statue loop going.

1. Lv 40: Arena (daily tickets; see [Weekly routine](weekly-routine.md) for the reset trick), Commercium by
   airship (market) [S-03][S-87]; the Skill Test damage dummy [S-85].
2. Lv 44: second class change. Mage becomes **Sorcerer** (recommended for a solo account) or **Sage**; the
   choice is mae's and can be changed for free once a week from Lv 100 [S-03][R-054].
3. Lv 50 (our rule recorded 51 [R-011]): Fantomons. Set Main, Deployed and three Subs; start aptitudes on the
   Main [S-01]. Level only the four deployed [S-87]. See [Fantomons](../systems/fantomons.md).
4. Companions: take Premium Commissions for journals; raise every companion to 20 before anything else [S-01].
5. Relics: display the best four per element, raise resonance in all five elements, class element first [S-01].
6. Kingdom tool here is the Magic Drill. Top use: the Verdantglade side area with two Lost Goddess Statues
   behind one Boulder Blockade (the Mushroom Woods area) [S-01]; then Hidden Camp [R-151].
7. Mecha Summit dungeon (Hard 180K).

Exit: the voyage to Aqualis.

## Phase 3: Aqualis (level about 82 to 100)

1. Third class (Sorcerer to Archmage, Sage to Arcanist) at Lv 82 with total class level 48 [S-85]. See
   [Classes overview](../systems/classes-overview.md).
2. Ocean Palace Ruins dungeon (Hard 580K).
3. Kingdom tool: Water Mine. Keep buying one tool per kingdom-currency cycle. Map totals [S-85]: Cinder Ridge
   4 keys, 3 potions, 5 drills, 3 Water Mines; Aqualis 5 keys, 2 potions, 3 drills, 6 Water Mines.
4. Before Lv 100, everything you raise is permanent: skill levels, gear to +100, Fantomon levels, relic resonance
   to the cap. Do not hold back resources below 100 [S-03].

Exit: Lv 100.

## Phase 4: Season 1 end game (level 100 to about 124)

1. Lv 100: Aquorigin Shrine (Normal 930K, Hard 1.45M, Nightmare 2.05M), then Hellhold nine days later (Normal
   2.25M, Hard 2.75M, Nightmare 3.9M, Purgatory 6.2M) [S-01][S-03].
2. Lv 100 also opens the lossless class change: the Mage line can be re-chosen for free once a week [S-03].
3. Carry loop: meet the threshold, wear the kingdom's drop-bonus gear, show the Lucky Statue, run the highest
   difficulty with a carry, sell spare shards [S-01].
4. Ore into gear: the Season 1 gear rating is SSS with 2 Divine and 3 Mythic Hellhold pieces [S-02].
5. Target at least 104 Primostars (128 if possible) [S-01][S-02]. See [Astral Pact](../systems/astral-pact.md).
6. Stop pulling skills; bank Stellatie and vouchers for the Tier 4 pool [S-01].
7. Relic gacha toward 70% completion in each region [S-01].

Exit: the season rollover. Follow [Season playbook](season-playbook.md).

## Phases 5 to 8: every later season

The same four steps each season; details and checklists in [Season playbook](season-playbook.md).

| Step | What matters |
|---|---|
| Change-over | Bed EXP uncollected for 36 h, 300 stamina banked, 6 dungeon runs banked, tools and shards handled |
| Early (pre-season and first weeks) | Light the new kingdom's statues, then speed-ups; relic pool 1 with Dawnium; sand; reach the top rank (Champion III at Lv 120 in Season 2); new class tier (Lv 106), Adult Fantomon (Lv 108), tower (Lv 116) |
| Middle | A new dungeon every 13 days; arrive with the threshold met and 6 runs banked; ore and gear; realm tools 4244 or 4444 |
| Late | Gear enhancement for Primostars (Season 2 targets 680 and 800) |

Season 2 thresholds for planning: 2.35M at the start (Demonseal Gorge, Hard), 3.55M and Lv 130 by day 60, 6.2M by
day 73, 10M by day 86, 16.5M by day 99 [S-02].

Season 3 (Aethyris) follows Season 2 on the guide's calendar [S-02]. eog's server-day roadmap, which projects the

|---|---|---|
| 1 / 4 / 12 | Verdantglade / Cinder Ridge / Aqualis (Season 1) | Tier 2 at Lv 44, Tier 3 at Lv 82 |
| 24 / 33 | Aquorigin Shrine (Lv 100) / Hellhold | - |
| 47 | Loong Haven, Season 2 | Tier 4 at Lv 106 |
| 114 | Aethyris, Season 3 | Tier 5 at Lv 136 |
| 226 | Harpadia, Season 4 | Tier 6 at Lv 172 |
| 335 | Ignis, Season 5 (last power wall 1.8 billion) | Tier 7 at Lv 198 |

Days 1 to 113 agree with SOF's calendar; whether global keeps the Chinese schedule after Season 2 is not known ❓. The Chinese version of the game is about four
class tiers further on; what it shows about later kingdoms, class tiers and systems is collected in
[Future content](../systems/future-content.md). Treat it as forecast.
