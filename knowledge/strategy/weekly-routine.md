# Weekly routine and calendar events

<!-- index: Read for everything that happens weekly or on the season calendar: Arena reset, Guild Shop, Molly's Deals, Grand Treasure Hunt, Chaos Invasion, new dungeons -->

> **Summary:** five weekly habits and three calendar habits. Weekly: climb the Arena right after its reset, buy
> Fantomon materials in the Guild Shop, take up to 10 Molly's Deals, follow the SAVE / MUST BUY rhythm of the
> Saturday Grand Treasure Hunt, claim weekly achievements. Calendar: arrive at each new dungeon with the threshold
> met and 6 runs banked, buy only the Primal Forge after Chaos Invasion, and prepare the season change.
> **Applies:** all seasons (Arena and market from Lv 40)
> **Related:** [Daily routine](daily-routine.md), [Events](../systems/events.md), [Season playbook](season-playbook.md),
> data in [events.json](../data/events.json) and [season-schedule.json](../data/season-schedule.json)

## Every week

| When | What | How | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday 8:00 server time | Weekly achievement quests reset | Claim in Menu > Quest > Achievement before the reset | Free rewards | [R-099] |
| Arena week end, then reset (weekday ❓) | Save Arena tickets late in the week; spend them all right after the reset | The fresh ladder is bots; a high early rank earns points all week, and rank 1 is possible | Weekly Arena points | [S-01] |
| Weekly shop refresh | Guild Shop: Fantomon aptitude materials and summon tokens first | Guild Tokens from donations, AFK gathering, Conquest | Permanent power | [S-01] |
| Any day, check a few times | Molly's Deals: at the Home gate, friends with an orange house mark | Up to 10 deals a week: discounted Stellatie, Covenite, relic badges | Cheapest pulls in the game | [S-01] |
| Saturday | Grand Treasure Hunt | Alternate weeks: SAVE (buy nothing big), MUST BUY (Primal Gem or Lucky Statue) | The two unique relics | [S-01][S-02] |
| Sunday, alternating ❓ | Void Rift / Chaos Invasion | Party of 4 where possible | Event currency | [Events](../systems/events.md) |
| Once a week from Lv 100 | Free lossless class change (does not stack) | Only when mae wants a different class | Class flexibility | [S-03] |

## Grand Treasure Hunt rhythm

- The unique relics rotate: one MUST BUY week sells the Primal Gem, the next one the Lucky Statue, with SAVE
  weeks between [S-01][S-02]. The full Season 2 calendar is in [events.json](../data/events.json).
- Lucky Statue: shows "Dungeon: 2x Rating Reward Rate 10%"; keep it in the displayed relic line (+20% from
  display) whenever a dungeon is run [S-01]. Buying order when both are missing: Lucky Statue first if dungeons
  are being run daily, since it pays back in shards ❓ (our judgement).
- On SAVE weeks the featured items (Tier 4 skill fragments, a Loong Haven Mythic relic choice) are skipped unless
  the event currency is clearly above what the next MUST BUY needs ❓.

## Chaos Invasion

1. Fight in a party: one damage dealer and three supports, rotating so each player gets a damage run [S-01].
2. The boss has very low armor: burst and team buffs win [S-01].
3. After the event the Mysterious Merchant appears at Home: buy **only the Primal Forge** until it is maxed; if
   it cannot be afforded, dismiss him and keep the currency for the next event [S-01].

## Wheel of Destiny choices

Skill Shard Vouchers; then Gateway Keys and Auroral Elemental Badges; then Legendary Auroral Badges, Covenite,
Bond Trinkets; then Legendary Fantomon materials, 2,888 Dawnium, extra Lucky Tickets [S-01].
