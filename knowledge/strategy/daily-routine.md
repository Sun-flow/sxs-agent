# Daily routine

<!-- index: Read to know everything a day should contain, in order, why the order matters, and which bot loop covers each step -->

> **Summary:** one pass after the daily reset (8:00 server time, UTC-5 [R-024], which is the 13:00 UTC reset eog lists [S-85]) covers the claims and the
> limited attempts; a second short pass late in the day spends leftover stamina and collects idle output. Order
> matters in three places: light new statues before speed-ups, advance rank before anything that gives EXP, and
> spend Dawnium from the top of the priority list.
> **Applies:** all seasons; rows marked with a level appear once that feature is unlocked
> **Related:** [Weekly routine](weekly-routine.md), [Dawnium plan](dawnium-plan.md), [Stamina and Material Realms](stamina-and-realms.md),
> [Dungeons: content checklist](../systems/dungeons.md#daily-and-weekly-content-checklist), `docs/step4-dailies.md`

## The daily quest list

Six tasks: log in, 2 Vendor Booth purchases, spend 30 stamina, Material Realms x5, Fantasia Ascent once, Conquest
once [R-128]. Two of them need actions the bot's rules do not allow yet (Vendor purchases) or that the guides
shape (realm runs): see [conflicts](../conflicts.md).

## What may be skipped on purpose

| Skip | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Dungeon runs | The two days before a new dungeon opens | Bank 6 runs for the opening day [S-02] |
| Material Realm tools (not the free daily runs) | Until the kingdom's top rank | Rewards scale with rank [S-01][S-02] |
| Bed collection | The last 36 h of a season | The EXP lands in the new season [S-02] |
| Free speed-up and Temporal Crystals | Until the day's (or the new kingdom's) statues are lit | Higher rate first [S-02][S-03] |
| Event shop purchases | SAVE weeks of the Grand Treasure Hunt | Afford the unique relics [S-01] |
