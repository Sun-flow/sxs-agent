## Standing rules for a Mage

1. One loadout per element; switch to the element the enemies are weak to. Wind beats Water, Water beats Fire,
   Fire beats Wind; the wrong element does half damage or none [S-03].
2. Ascension is permanent, levels above 100 are not: push a skill to its next quality step, then move to the next
   skill [S-03].
3. Damage affixes for the Sorcerer line: Crit Rate + Crit DMG, stat-to-ATK conversions, Crit Rate + Accuracy,
   Technique damage to debuffed enemies [S-02].
4. Fantomon: Main for aptitudes (Nyxarchon: Crit Rate, Accuracy), Deployed for its skill [S-01][S-02].
5. Gems, Sorcerer line: red or purple, yellow, green; Sage line: red or purple, orange or green [S-01].
