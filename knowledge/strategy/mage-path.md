## Sorcerer or Sage: what the tier list adds

| Tier | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |

| Sorcerer line | Archmage A | Destroyer A | Magister A | Cataclyst A | Voidcaller S | Obliterator A |
| Sage line | Arcanist B | Dominator B | Prophet S | Oracle C | Ascendant B | Fateweaver B |

Grades are eog's [S-85] (tier C, later tiers read from the CN server). They support the standing recommendation:
the Sorcerer line never drops below A, while the Sage line peaks once (Prophet) and eog flags Oracle as a tier to
avoid. The Sorcerer line also has the highest ATK weight of all lines at every tier [S-80]. The choice stays
mae's [R-054].

## Standing rules for a Mage

1. One loadout per element; switch to the element the enemies are weak to. Wind beats Water, Water beats Fire,
   Fire beats Wind; the wrong element does half damage or none [S-03].
2. Ascension is permanent, levels above 100 are not: push a skill to its next quality step, then move to the next
   skill [S-03].
3. Damage affixes for the Sorcerer line: Crit Rate + Crit DMG, stat-to-ATK conversions, Crit Rate + Accuracy,
   Technique damage to debuffed enemies [S-02].
4. Fantomon: Main for aptitudes (Nyxarchon: Crit Rate, Accuracy), Deployed for its skill [S-01][S-02].
5. Gems, Sorcerer line: red or purple, yellow, green; Sage line: red or purple, orange or green [S-01].
6. Mastery maths: 40 Elemental Mastery is +1% damage. On eog's damage dummy Wind's Delight tested best for a
   Sorcerer, then Water Assault; Stonechief Summon worst [S-85]. Test your own loadout at Home > Character >
   Skills > Test from Lv 40.
7. One element or all five: eog tells free players to commit to one element (Sorcerer Light or Fire, Sage Dark)
   [S-85]; SOF says class element first, then all five [S-01]. This page keeps SOF; the two agree on what comes
   first.
