# Mage (Tier 1)

<!-- index: Read for the Mage's skills, early builds and the recommended free-to-play path from level 1 through Sorcerer or Sage to Tier 4 -->

> **Summary:** The Mage is the ranged starting class and the one our account plays (mae's choice, R-054). It fights with Water, Fire and Wind spells, so its damage depends on matching the enemy's element. Until Lv 44 run an AoE Water or Wind loadout with Mana Surge, Unstable Aura and Gale Shield, level everything with Quick Upgrade, and bank Stellatie pulls toward the 200-prayer Legendary pick. At Lv 44 the Mage becomes a Sorcerer (damage) or a Sage (support); this page recommends Sorcerer for a solo-progressing account, and the choice can be reversed for free at Lv 100.
> **Applies:** early game (Lv 1-44); Mage skills stay usable on both Mage lines in all seasons
> **Related:** [Classes overview](../systems/classes-overview.md), [Skills](../systems/skills.md), [Sorcerer](sorcerer.md), [Sage](sage.md), [Archmage](archmage.md), [Arcanist](arcanist.md), [Tier 4](tier-4.md), [Relics](../systems/relics.md), [Fantomons](../systems/fantomons.md), rules R-054, R-037, R-104, R-115

## Role

- A ranged spellcaster that deals magical damage and can also heal, control and summon [S-03].
- Base stat weighting: HP 500, ATK 1400, DEF 500, SPD 600, against the Warrior's 900 / 600 / 900 / 600: more than twice the attack, about half the durability [S-10].
- The Mage path is the more complicated of the two starts, for two reasons [S-03]:
  1. **Elements.** Wind beats Water, Water beats Fire, Fire beats Wind. An enemy halves damage from the element it beats and may be immune to it (elemental slimes), and takes much more from the element that beats it. A Mage needs a loadout per element.
  2. **Build variety.** Attacks, buffs, heals and summons compete for 4 technique and 4 charm slots; swapping two skills changes how the class plays.

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Highest attack; AoE from the first hours (Water Assault, Blazing Fire Ring, Wind's Delight) [S-03] | Fragile: lowest HP and DEF [S-10][S-04] |
| Range of 3 to 6 grids keeps it out of melee [S-03] | Wrong element = half damage or none [S-03] |
| Control: Frozen, Immobilize, pull, knockback [S-03] | Slower start than the Warrior; reviewers call the beginner Mage underwhelming [S-13] |
| Both Tier 4 forms are rated S [S-13] | Needs more loadout management than a Warrior [S-03] |

## Becoming a Mage, and what comes next

- The class is chosen in the quest "Crossroads of Classes" at Originisle V after the Guardian Colossi boss: the Chief offers "Ascend to Mage", "Change Class" or "Leave". The bot always picks Leave; mae chose Mage [R-054].
- Next step: **Lv 44, Cinder Ridge**, with Mage class level 18 (class level comes from Class Badges, which come from owning Mage skills) [S-03][S-10], R-117. Choose [Sorcerer](sorcerer.md) or [Sage](sage.md).
- The early Warrior-or-Mage choice matters little: the lossless class change at Lv 100 converts all pulled skills to any other class [S-03].

## Recommended builds (Tier 1)

Builds are tier D guidance [S-04]; the skill texts they rely on are from Fandom [S-03]. Use whatever you have pulled: a build is a target, not a requirement.

| Build | Techniques (cast order) | Charms | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water AoE (default) | Water Assault, Ice Spike, Iron Thorn (or Cyclone to group first), Water Shot | Water to Ice, Mana Surge, Unstable Aura, Gale Shield | Levelling, map clearing; freezes and immobilises. Weak into Wind enemies |
| Wind multi-hit | Cyclone, Wind's Delight, Tempest Sphere, Iron Thorn | Unstable Aura, Wind's Whisper, Mana Surge, Gale Shield | Bosses and single targets (many hits trigger Unstable Aura); use against Water enemies |
| Fire | Blazing Fire Ring, Fireball, Flame Jet, Cyclone | Heart of Flame, Flaming Path, Mana Surge, Gale Shield | Against Wind enemies. Blazing Fire Ring needs you within 2 grids of the pack, so keep Gale Shield |
| Mixed "what I have" | Best AoE of each element you own | Elemental Mystery, Mana Surge, Unstable Aura, Gale Shield | General use until a full element set exists; Elemental Mystery boosts every element |
| Party support | Healing Touch, Void Blessing, Iron Thorn, Stonechief Summon | Tough Soul, Insight, Elemental Body, Gale Shield | Only in group content; useless solo [S-14] |

Notes:
- AoE beats single target through the early and mid game because fights are packs [S-04].
- Curse Resonance adds Technique damage per ailment on the target (up to 3), so it fits the Water build once Frozen and Immobilize land reliably [S-03].
- PvP (Arena): Wind knockback and action delay deny melee enemies their turns [S-03]; at Tier 1 that means Unstable Aura, Gale Shield, Cyclone, Iron Thorn.

## Relic element

- Raise the element your loadout uses first; in the end all five elements are levelled [S-01]. Our rule: raise relics element by element in bands, the character's highest affinity first [R-105].
- For a Mage heading to Sorcerer: Fire is better in PvE than PvP, Light equally good in both, Wind is the PvP element, Water is better in 4v4 [S-01]. For one heading to Sage: Dark [S-01].
- See [Relics](../systems/relics.md).

## Fantomon

- Fantomons unlock at Lv 50 [S-11][S-15] ❓, so a Tier 1 Mage has none. Later, as a damage dealer: main Nyxarchon or an Attack-type Fantomon (aptitudes give Crit Rate and Accuracy) [S-01]. See [Fantomons](../systems/fantomons.md).

## Gear, gems, companions

- Gear affixes worth keeping come later (Sorcerer or Sage list) [S-02]; early on just equip the highest-power piece and enhance evenly (R-088, R-092). See [Gear](../systems/gear.md).

## Recommended path for a free-to-play Mage from level 1

This is the path for our account. Spending Dawnium is allowed in advice; real money is not. Class buttons stay mae's (R-054, R-117).

### Stage 1: Lv 1-44, Mage (Verdantglade, then Cinder Ridge)

1. **Skills first, every session.** Fill all open slots, Quick Upgrade, ascend anything with "!" [R-080][R-104][R-115].
2. **Pull only in x10 on the Standard Prayer** when mae runs the script [R-093]. Each 200 prayers gives a Legendary of choice: for a Mage take **Water Assault** first if not owned (main AoE through Tier 2, still used at Tier 3 [S-04][S-12]), then **Wind's Delight** or **Blazing Fire Ring** [S-14] ❓.
3. **Loadouts:** Water AoE as default; add a Wind and a Fire loadout as skills arrive, and switch to the element the zone's monsters are weak to [S-03].
4. **Do not spend Skill Shard Vouchers** on Tier 1 skills [S-15] ❓[S-01]. Natural duplicate shards are fine to use.
5. **Class level:** claim Class Badges and level the Mage class; class level 18 is needed for Tier 2 [S-10], R-117.
6. Everything else that raises power (rank at the level cap, gear bands, relics) follows the roadmap pages in `strategy/`.

### Stage 2: Lv 44, choose Sorcerer or Sage

| Question | Sorcerer | Sage |
|---|---|---|
| Solo story and map clearing (what the bot does most) | Best: raw AoE, rarely blocked, steady damage [S-03] | Slow early; "can't push content" is the common complaint [S-03] |
| Party value | Ranged damage; many players pick it | Mandatory healer in party content; always wanted [S-13] |
| Survivability | Most fragile of the four [S-03] | Higher base stats, heals itself [S-03] |
| Free-to-play outlook | Low gear entry barrier, "safest long-term pick" [S-11] ❓ | Highest ceiling for heavy spenders; weak without investment [S-11][S-14] ❓ |
| Tier 3 / Tier 4 rating | Archmage A, Destroyer S [S-13] | Arcanist S, Dominator S ("most complete class") [S-13][S-11] |
| Relic element | Fire or Light for PvE [S-01] | Dark [S-01] |

**Recommendation: Sorcerer**, because this account progresses alone through the kingdom story, where clear speed matters and every class must be a damage dealer anyway [S-03]. A Sage becomes the stronger solo class only from Tier 3 (Arcanist, Lv 82) onward [S-04][S-13]. The decision is cheap to reverse: at Lv 100 a free lossless change converts every Sorcerer skill into a Sage skill of the same tier and rarity, or the reverse [S-03]. mae decides; the bot stops with `needs_decision` at the class prompt [R-054].

### Stage 3: Lv 44-82, Sorcerer (Cinder Ridge to Aqualis)

- Pick one element as the main build and raise its relic resonance first: **Fire** for PvE clear speed or **Light** as the all-round choice [S-01]. Keep a second element loadout for resistant enemies. Details: [Sorcerer](sorcerer.md).
- The Premium (pink) Prayer banner opens around here; Covenite pulls target your class branch [S-11][S-15] ❓. See [Gacha](../systems/gacha.md).
- Fantomons open at Lv 50: main an Attack type; Nyxarchon when affordable [S-01].
- If the Sage route was taken instead: [Sage](sage.md), Dark relics, Erosion build for solo play.

### Stage 4: Lv 82-106, Archmage (Aqualis, Season 1 end)

- Light and Elemental Harmony builds lead, Fire close behind [S-12] ❓; details in [Archmage](archmage.md).
- Start using Skill Shard Vouchers, on skills that persist (Radiance Sear, Mana Surge, the main nuke of your element) and only up to a quality step [S-01][S-03].
- Lv 100: the lossless class change unlocks. Use the free weekly Slate only if mae wants a different class; there is an 8-hour undo [S-03].
- In the last weeks of a season stop pulling skills and save Stellatie for Tier 4 [S-01].

### Stage 5: Lv 106+, Destroyer (Loong Haven, Season 2) and beyond

- Tier 4 at Lv 106 [S-02]. Spend the saved pulls on the new pool. See [Tier 4](tier-4.md#destroyer-4th-sorcerer).
- Each later season adds a tier (Tier 5 is the Magister) [S-10][S-11]; repeat: bank pulls before the tier, ascend persistent skills, keep ascension (permanent) ahead of levels (seasonal) [S-03].
