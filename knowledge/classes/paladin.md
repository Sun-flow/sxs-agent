# Paladin (Tier 3, Knight line)

<!-- index: Read for the Paladin (Lv 82-106): what is known of the 3rd Knight; reference only, our account is a Mage -->

> **Summary:** The Paladin is the "3rd Knight": a stronger tank with almost no offensive gain, rated B. Its Legendaries include Star Shattering Slash and Holy Redemption. Builds and gear follow the Knight page. It promotes to Guardian at Lv 106.
> **Applies:** Lv 82-106 (Aqualis, end of Season 1)
> **Related:** [Knight](knight.md), [Tier 4: Guardian](tier-4.md#guardian-4th-knight), [Classes overview](../systems/classes-overview.md)

## Role

- "The core of a team's defense": where its shield rests, a no-fly zone is established [S-03]. In-game tagline: smite foes with sacred radiance and stand as a bulwark [S-03]. Fandom also calls it "Heavy Knight" [S-03].
- Stronger tanking, almost no offensive gain; restrictive solo; rated B [S-13][S-11].
- Base stat weighting: HP 2400, ATK 1600, DEF 2400, SPD 1800 [S-10]. Class stat: Block [S-10].
- Its Legendaries include Star Shattering Strike/Slash and Holy Redemption; the class-change loadouts let you favour either an offensive set or the classic taunt and "shield boomerang" (Ricocheting Shield) set [S-03]. Both Paladin Legendary Charms are considered weak [S-03].
- Fandom's Paladin page shows the Duelist tables unchanged [S-03].

## Unlock and next class

Lv 82 in Aqualis from Knight [S-11] ❓ (Knight class level 16 per [S-10]; a Fandom example says Knight job level 18 [S-03]); next Guardian at Lv 106 [S-02].
