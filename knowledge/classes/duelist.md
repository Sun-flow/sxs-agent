# Duelist (Tier 2, Warrior line)

<!-- index: Read for the Duelist (Lv 44-82): skills, Multi-Strike and Spellblade builds, stats, relic element and gear affixes; reference for match-ups and class change -->

> **Summary:** The Duelist is the Warrior's damage branch: high Crit Rate, multi-hit combos, lifesteal and cheat-death, with swingy damage because its low Accuracy gets blocked. It is the best duellist and solo story class at Tier 2 and the most played class. Fire relics, Attack Fantomon, crit gear. It promotes to Berserker at Lv 82.
> **Applies:** Lv 44-82; Duelist skills stay usable on the line in all seasons
> **Related:** [Warrior](warrior.md), [Berserker](berserker.md), [Tier 4: Conqueror](tier-4.md#conqueror-4th-duelist), [Classes overview](../systems/classes-overview.md), [Skills](../systems/skills.md), [Arena and PvP](../systems/arena-and-pvp.md)

## Role

- Sheds heavy armour for agility; explosive bursts and strong recovery turn near-fatal wounds into counter-attacks [S-03].
- Elements: Physical, Dark and Fire; Gauntlets off-hand [S-11] ❓. Class stat: Crit Rate [S-03][S-10].
- High Crit Rate but low Accuracy: crits deal 150% and more, blocked hits are cut hard, so output is unstable, 50% to 300% per hit [S-03].
- Base stat weighting: HP 1000, ATK 1500, DEF 1000, SPD 1500 [S-10].
- Often more than half the player base [S-11] ❓.

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Best 1v1 PvP and solo story progress at Tier 2; top free-to-play pick in launch-era lists [S-13][S-14] | Weak AoE and slow dungeon clears until Berserker [S-11][S-13] |
| Indomitable Will (survive a lethal hit) plus lifesteal [S-03] | Damage swings with Block; needs Accuracy [S-03] |
| Scales with crit and combos rather than rare pulls [S-11] ❓ | Crowded class: parties rarely need another [S-03] |

## Unlock and next class

## Builds

Fandom names two builds [S-03]: **Multi-Strike** (many hits, Charms do most of the damage) and **Spellblade** (Techniques and direct combat). "Dark Duelist" is a named build using Dark skills; "Crit Duelist" is the standard style, "Accuracy Duelist" and "Block Duelist" rarer ones [S-03].

| Build | Techniques | Charms | Use | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AoE / general | Flame Aura, Whirlwind Slash, Sunset Sword, Flash Dash | Block Awareness, Indomitable Will, Blade of Lament, Counter Blade | Levelling, dungeons | [S-11] ❓ |
| Single target | Leap Attack, Flame Aura, Heavy Impact, Flash Dash (or Lion Combo / Edge Strike) | same | Bosses | [S-11] ❓ |
| Aggressive burst | Sunset Sword, Flash Dash, Darkness Descends, Fire Slash | Indomitable Will, Blazing Clash, Crit Mastery, Frame of Battles | PvP, bosses | [S-04][S-11] ❓ |

- Frame of Battles and Potential Vitality both scale per 15% HP lost (up to 5 stacks): the lower the Duelist's HP, the less damage it takes and the more it is healed [S-03].
- Blazing Clash adds damage per 4 Techniques used, up to 3 stacks: it rewards long fights [S-03].

## Stat priorities

Crit Rate, Crit DMG, Accuracy, Block [S-11] ❓; companions at Lv 50 for Crit Rate and Accuracy [S-01]. A later guide lists Speed, Crit Rate, Crit DMG, Elemental Mastery, Physical Mastery [S-11] ❓.

## Relic element

Duelist: **Fire** [S-01].

## Fantomon

Main Nyxarchon or an Attack type (Crit Rate, Accuracy aptitudes) [S-01]; Season 2 order as for all DPS [S-02].

## Top gear affixes

Crit Rate + Crit DMG; Speed/HP/Defense (converted) to Attack; Crit Rate + Accuracy; Dodge chance (from Loong Haven); Technique damage dealt to enemies with debuffs [S-02].

## Gems and companions

Gems: Red/Purple, Yellow, Green [S-01]. Season 2 companions: Wuji, Ming (Crit Rate, DMG Boost), Jiangwang (Accuracy, DMG Boost) [S-02].
