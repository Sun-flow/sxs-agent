# Archmage (Tier 3, Sorcerer line)

<!-- index: Read when playing the Archmage (Lv 82-106): Tier 3 Sorcerer skills, element builds and their ranking, stats, gear, and preparing for Destroyer -->

> **Summary:** The Archmage is the "3rd Sorcerer": the same four elements with a signature nuke for each (Divine Wrath, Meteoric Flames, Howling Hurricane, Aqua Vortex) and the charms Radiance Sear and Rapid Cast that go into every build. Light and the four-element Elemental Harmony build are rated strongest, Fire next. Tier 3 is also where the real build system starts and where Skill Shard Vouchers begin to be worth spending. It promotes to Destroyer at Lv 106. Almost everything on this page beyond the class description comes from tier D guides and is marked ❓.
> **Applies:** Lv 82-106 (Aqualis, end of Season 1); skills stay usable in later tiers
> **Related:** [Sorcerer](sorcerer.md), [Tier 4: Destroyer](tier-4.md#destroyer-4th-sorcerer), [Mage](mage.md), [Skills](../systems/skills.md), [Classes overview](../systems/classes-overview.md)

## Role

- "An architect of elemental truth" who can dismantle enemy formations with a single incantation [S-03]. In-game tagline: unleash the raw elements and engulf enemies in a storm of mana [S-03].
- Same role as the Sorcerer, stronger AoE. Rated A at Tier 3 (S at Tier 2) because other classes catch up [S-13][S-11].
- Base stat weighting: HP 1650, ATK 2950, DEF 1600, SPD 2000 [S-10].
- Fandom's Archmage page exists but its skill tables are an unedited copy of the Duelist tables, so it gives no Archmage skills [S-03].

## Unlock and next class

- Unlock: Lv 82 in Aqualis from Sorcerer [S-11] ❓, with Sorcerer class level 16 and total class level 48 [S-10].
- At Lv 100 the lossless class change opens [S-03].
- Next: Destroyer at Lv 106 in Loong Haven (Archmage class level 19, total 89) [S-02][S-10].

## Builds

Ranking of element builds at Tier 3 (Roonby, 2026-06-04, described by its author as provisional) [S-12] ❓: **S** Elemental Harmony, Light. **A** Fire. **B** Wind, Freeze (Water).

| Build | Techniques | Charms | Use | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AoE (general) | Divine Wrath, Howling Hurricane, Meteoric Flames, Lightning Chain | Rapid Cast, Void Bubble, Mana Surge, Radiance Sear | Dungeons, map clearing; one nuke per element | [S-11] ❓ |
| Single target | Divine Wrath, Howling Hurricane, Meteoric Flames, Wind's Delight (replaces Lightning Chain) | same | Bosses | [S-11] ❓ |
| Light | Divine Wrath, Afflicting Stars, Light of Dawn (+ Lightning Chain) | Radiance Sear, Incarnation of Light, Rapid Cast (+ Mana Surge) | Highest single-element damage; Divine Wrath prioritises large targets | [S-12] ❓ |
| Fire | Meteoric Flames, Fire Blast (+ Fiery Star Trail) | Heart of Flame, Radiance Sear, Rapid Cast, Raging Wildfire | PvE AoE; do not add Flaming Path next to Raging Wildfire | [S-12] ❓ |
| Wind | Howling Hurricane, Tempest Sphere, Wind Clone, Wind's Delight | Wind's Shadow, Rapid Cast, Radiance Sear, Wind Affinity | Mobility and control, PvP; lower raw damage | [S-12] ❓ |
| Freeze (Water) | Aqua Vortex, Water Assault, ice control (Frosty Nova, Ice Spike) | Dew's Blessing, Radiance Sear, Rapid Cast (+ Water to Ice) | Boss lock: Aqua Vortex applies Wet, which raises freeze chance; lowest damage | [S-12] ❓ |
| Elemental Harmony | One nuke per element (the AoE build above) | Elemental Harmony, Radiance Sear, Rapid Cast, Mana Surge | Strongest overall and best scaling per the guide; no element swapping needed | [S-12] ❓ |

Defensive charm options [S-12] ❓:
1. Frog Guard: damage reduction, then HP restored when it ends; needs techniques with cooldowns.
2. Void Bubble ("Bubble Shield"): shield and Dodge below 50% HP [S-03].
3. Repelling Wind: pushes attackers away; PvP and melee.

For this account (solo PvE): the AoE / Elemental Harmony line-up is the least demanding because it needs no loadout swapping; Light is the pick if Light relics were raised first [S-01][S-12].

## Relic element, Fantomon, gear, gems, companions

Unchanged from the Sorcerer:

- Relic element: Fire (PvE), Light (both), Wind (PvP), Water (4v4) [S-01].
- Fantomon: main Nyxarchon or an Attack type; Adult evolution unlocks at Lv 108; Pandarial becomes the deployed Fantomon in Season 2 [S-01][S-02].
- Gear affixes: Crit Rate + Crit DMG; Speed/HP/Defense to Attack; Crit Rate + Accuracy; Flash (from Loong Haven); Technique damage to debuffed enemies [S-02].
- Gems: Red/Purple, Yellow, Green [S-01].
- Companions: Crit Rate and Accuracy companions first [S-01]; one guide names Yui, Shin and Akane [S-12] ❓.

## Getting ready for Destroyer (end of Season 1)

1. In the final weeks stop pulling skills and save Stellatie and Covenite for the Tier 4 pool [S-01].
2. Spend Skill Shard Vouchers only on skills that carry forward: Radiance Sear is still in Tier 5 builds, as are Howling Hurricane and Meteoric Flames [S-11] ❓; stop each at a quality step [S-03].
3. Keep ascending (permanent) rather than chasing skill levels above 100 (reset) [S-03].
4. Push gear level for the Astral Pact rating [S-01]. See [Astral Pact](../systems/astral-pact.md).
