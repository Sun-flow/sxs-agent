# Arcanist (Tier 3, Sage line)

<!-- index: Read when playing the Arcanist (Lv 82-106): Tier 3 Sage skills, Erosion DPS / healer / summoner builds, stats, gear, and preparing for Dominator -->

> **Summary:** The Arcanist is the "3rd Sage" and the point where the Sage line becomes a top class: Erosion gets a finisher that sets off every stack at once (Shadow of Termination), a cheat-death charm (Shadow Vengeance), and the healer gets a healing summon and Overhealing. It is rated S at Tier 3. Run pure Erosion for solo and damage content and the healer loadout in parties. It promotes to Dominator at Lv 106. Skill details here come from tier D guides and are marked ❓.
> **Applies:** Lv 82-106 (Aqualis, end of Season 1); skills stay usable in later tiers
> **Related:** [Sage](sage.md), [Tier 4: Dominator](tier-4.md#dominator-4th-sage), [Mage](mage.md), [Skills](../systems/skills.md), [Classes overview](../systems/classes-overview.md)

## Role

- "The grand strategist": weaves summons, healing and crowd control and dictates the rhythm of the fight [S-03]. In-game tagline: wield summoning and charms to control the tide of battle [S-03].
- Rated S at Tier 3: gains situations of full damage and death immunity and competitive damage skills [S-13][S-11].
- Base stat weighting: 2050 in each of HP, ATK, DEF and SPD [S-10].
- Fandom's Arcanist page is a placeholder with the Duelist skill tables [S-03].

## Unlock and next class

- Unlock: Lv 82 in Aqualis from Sage [S-11] ❓, with Sage class level 16 and total class level 48 [S-10].
- Next: Dominator at Lv 106 in Loong Haven (Arcanist class level 19, total 89) [S-02][S-10].

How the Erosion build works [S-04][S-11] ❓, on top of the Tier 2 engine [S-03]:
1. Apply Erosion with every Dark hit (Shadow Erosion 40% base chance; Dark Bullet 50%) [S-03].
2. Linked Misfortune gives a 60% chance to apply Erosion again whenever it is applied.
3. Stack as many as possible, then cast Shadow of Termination last: it sets off all stacks at once.
4. Shadow Vengeance is the safety net: on a lethal hit the caster becomes a Shadow for a turn (no damage taken, shorter cooldowns, more damage).
5. Effect Hit Rate decides whether the "base chances" land, so it is the first stat.

Healer notes: Overhealing triggers from Healing Touch but not from the Waterling's heals; choose Healing Touch + Overhealing when the party needs more healing, Waterling when one heal source is enough [S-16] ❓.

## Relic element, Fantomon, gear, gems, companions

Unchanged from the Sage:

- Relic element: Dark [S-01].
- Fantomon: support main by preference (Support aptitudes: DMG Boost, DMG Reduction); damage Arcanist: Nyxarchon / Attack type. Season 2 support path: Aegiswing to Adult, Pandarial to Immortal with aptitudes for Healing Boost [S-01][S-02].
- Gear affixes: general: Technique damage to debuffed enemies, Speed/HP/Defense to Attack, Crit Rate + Crit DMG; DPS: Crit Rate + Accuracy; healer: Block + Block Efficiency, DMG reduction + healing boost [S-02].
- Gems: Red/Purple, Orange/Green [S-01].
- Companions: healer by preference (Healing Boost at companion Lv 100) [S-01]; one guide names Aqua, Suimo and Suzu for Healing Boost [S-04] ❓, and SOF lists Suimo in Season 2 [S-02].

## Getting ready for Dominator (end of Season 1)

1. Stop pulling skills in the last weeks; bank pulls for Tier 4 [S-01].
2. Vouchers into skills still used at Tier 4: Shadow of Termination, Abyssal Hand, Frenzy Totem, Resurrection, Shadow Vengeance, Mana Surge appear in Dominator builds [S-16] ❓.
3. Ascension is permanent, levels above 100 are not [S-03].
