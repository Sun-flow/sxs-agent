# Warrior (Tier 1)

<!-- index: Read for the Warrior's skills and early builds, and for what a Warrior-line class inherits; our account is a Mage, so this is reference -->

> **Summary:** The Warrior is the melee starting class: high HP and DEF, Physical damage that nothing resists and nothing is weak to, and short range. It is the simpler start. At Lv 44 it becomes a Duelist (damage) or a Knight (tank). Our account plays Mage, so this page matters for party play, PvP match-ups and a possible lossless class change after Lv 100.
> **Applies:** early game (Lv 1-44); Warrior skills stay usable on both Warrior lines in all seasons
> **Related:** [Duelist](duelist.md), [Knight](knight.md), [Mage](mage.md), [Classes overview](../systems/classes-overview.md), [Skills](../systems/skills.md)

## Role

- A strong fighter with high HP and DEF that endures heavy hits and excels at melee pursuit and counter-blocking [S-03].
- Base stat weighting: HP 900, ATK 600, DEF 900, SPD 600 [S-10].
- The simpler of the two starts: mostly Physical damage, so whatever you equip works on every enemy; solid defence [S-03].

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| No element to manage: Physical is never resisted [S-03] | Never gets a weakness bonus either [S-03] |
| Durable; more beginner-friendly [S-03][S-11] | Short range; trouble with ranged, evasive enemies apart from one pull (Gravity Pull) [S-03] |
| Strong Legendary set from the start (Lion Combo, Heavy Impact, Whirlwind Slash) [S-14] | Tank branch is slow solo [S-13] |

## Unlock and next class

- Chosen at Originisle V in "Crossroads of Classes" [R-054].
- Lv 44, Cinder Ridge, Warrior class level 18: [Duelist](duelist.md) or [Knight](knight.md) [S-03][S-10].

## Builds (Tier 1)

Tier D guidance [S-14][S-17][S-04][S-11] ❓.

| Build | Techniques | Charms | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Burst / levelling | Leap Attack (opener, Armor Break), Lion Combo, Heavy Impact, Whirlwind Slash | Blade of Lament (lifesteal), Counter Blade, Block Awareness, Insightful Eye | Solo story; bosses |
| AoE clear | Gravity Pull, Whirlwind Slash, Mountain Collapse, Quadrant Slash | Blade of Lament, Counter Blade, Warrior's Essence, Crystal Armor | Packs |
| Tanky | Luminous Shield, Whirlwind Slash, Mountain Collapse, Heavy Impact | Iron Fortress, Pure Protection, Block Awareness, Counter Blade | Party frontline before Knight |

- Blade of Lament is the usual first 200-prayer pick for a Warrior (lifesteal) [S-04] ❓.
- Boiling Bloodlust (Crit Rate and Crit DMG self-buff) matters mainly on bosses [S-14] ❓.

## Stat priorities

- Tier 1-2 gear: Physical Mastery and ATK%; crit is not worth heavy investment yet [S-11] ❓.
- Then by branch: Crit Rate, Crit DMG, Accuracy for the Duelist; Block, Block Efficiency, HP, DEF for the Knight [S-01][S-02].

## Relic element, Fantomon, gear, companions

Depends on the branch: Duelist: Fire relics, Attack Fantomon; Knight: Water or Light relics, Aegiswing or a Tank Fantomon [S-01]. See [Duelist](duelist.md) and [Knight](knight.md).
