# Tier 4 classes: Conqueror, Guardian, Destroyer, Dominator (and the Tier 5 preview)

<!-- index: Read from Lv 100 onward: what each Tier 4 class does, known skills and builds, how to prepare for the Lv 106 change, and what is known of Tier 5 -->

> **Summary:** Tier 4 unlocks at Lv 106 in Loong Haven at the start of Season 2. Each line gets its most flexible form: Conqueror (physical combo or elemental hybrid), Guardian (tank with Water or Light offence), Destroyer (Light and Fire AoE plus a defence-breaking utility) and Dominator (Shadow Priest, pure healer or summoner). Both Mage-line classes are rated S. Prepare by banking Prayer pulls and Skill Shard Vouchers before the change. Skill details are mostly tier D and marked ❓.
> **Applies:** Season 2+ (Lv 106+)
> **Related:** [Archmage](archmage.md), [Arcanist](arcanist.md), [Berserker](berserker.md), [Paladin](paladin.md), [Classes overview](../systems/classes-overview.md), [Skills](../systems/skills.md), [Seasons](../systems/seasons.md), [Fantomons](../systems/fantomons.md)

## Unlocking Tier 4

- **Level 106**, after entering Loong Haven's "Unrakumotogo" in the Dragon Realm, where the mentor Mei guides the ascension [S-03]; SOF: "you unlock your T4 class change at level 106" [S-02].
- Class-level gate: Tier 3 class at class level 19, total class level 89 [S-10][S-80][S-85].
- Server timing: about Day 47, together with Season 2 [S-11] ❓.
- Nearby unlocks: Adult Fantomon evolution at Lv 108; Demonbind Tower at Lv 116; maximum kingdom rank Champion III at Lv 120 [S-02].

### Preparing (last weeks of Season 1)

1. Stop pulling skills; save pull resources for Tier 4 [S-01].
2. Keep Skill Shard Vouchers for Tier 4 skills that persist: on the Duelist line the charm Piercing Assault is the example, worth all vouchers even if you change class at Tier 5 [S-01].
3. At Lv 100 the free weekly lossless class change is available: decide the Tier 4 class you want before spending vouchers [S-03].
4. Season-start routine (bed EXP, stamina, Material Realm tools): see [Seasons](../systems/seasons.md) [S-02].

## Dominator (4th Sage)

- "The highest dimension of battlefield dominance", with three paths [S-03]:
  - **Shadow Priest**: single-target Dark pressure with **Dark Star Burst**.
  - **Pure Healer**: **Rain of Revival** and **Phantom Light Strategy** keep the team alive.
  - **Summoner**: the very durable **Stone Emperor**, a living fortress.
- Described as the most complete class: handles most content without a specific team; rated S [S-11][S-13].
- Base stat weighting: 3050 in each of HP, ATK, DEF, SPD [S-10].
- A database site lists the techniques as Chaos Rune, Spirit Aegis, Rock Rex Summon and Rejuvenating Rain [S-10]; Rock Rex Summon is Fandom's "Stone Emperor" and Rejuvenating Rain its "Rain of Revival" (different translations).

## Conqueror (4th Duelist)

- "The overlord who has surpassed the laws of physics": blades resonate with Elemental Mastery; play the traditional Physical combo or an elemental hybrid of steel and spirit [S-03].
- Widely seen as the Duelist line's dominant tier; strong in PvP and on bosses; rated A because its burst identity plateaus before the Mage lines [S-11][S-13].
- sxswiki describes it as "no cooldowns / stun immunity" [S-04] ❓; Flickering Blade has no cooldown [S-11] ❓.
- Base stat weighting: HP 2650, ATK 3450, DEF 2700, SPD 3400 [S-10].

## Guardian (4th Knight)

- "Proof that defense can be the ultimate offense": keeps top shields and gains aggressive skills such as **Aqua Dance Blade** and **Luminous Sword Array**; choose the Water Knight or the Light Knight [S-03].
- Best pure frontline with more defensive and support tools; rated B for low damage [S-13][S-11].
- Base stat weighting: HP 3400, ATK 2600, DEF 3400, SPD 2800 [S-10].
