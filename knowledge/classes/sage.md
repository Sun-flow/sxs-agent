# Sage (Tier 2, Mage line)

<!-- index: Read when playing or choosing the Sage (Lv 44-82): skills, Erosion / direct damage / summoner builds and the healer style, stats, relic element, gear affixes, and when to move on to Arcanist -->

> **Summary:** The Sage is the Mage's support branch: the only line with group heals and a revive, plus Dark damage, the stacking Erosion debuff and summons. It has higher base stats than other classes but lower raw damage at Tier 2, so solo progress is slow until Tier 3. Play Erosion (Dark) for solo content and a healer loadout for parties; raise Dark relics first. It promotes to Arcanist at Lv 82.
> **Applies:** Lv 44-82 (Cinder Ridge to Aqualis); Sage skills stay usable on the line in all seasons
> **Related:** [Mage](mage.md), [Arcanist](arcanist.md), [Tier 4: Dominator](tier-4.md#dominator-4th-sage), [Sorcerer](sorcerer.md), [Classes overview](../systems/classes-overview.md), [Skills](../systems/skills.md), [Dungeons](../systems/dungeons.md)

## Role

- "The ultimate tactical polymath": summons, status effects and critical heals; heals allies with light and punishes enemies with darkness [S-03].
- More supportive and tactical than the Sorcerer, with the widest spread of party heals and buffs and a mix of minions. Raw damage is generally lower than every class except perhaps the Knight, but its party support is peerless [S-03].
- Uses a staff with an Orb off-hand; its damage element is Dark [S-11] ❓[S-01].
- No single class stat: higher base stats across the board (HP / ATK / DEF / SPD all 1250 in the relative table), with Crit Resistance and Healing Boost as traits [S-03][S-10][S-11]. Damage per hit ranges 50% to 200% [S-03].
- It lacks strong innate damage reduction, so do not stack Block; Crit, reliable damage boosts and damage reduction work well [S-03].

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Only class that heals a party, cleanses and revives (Resurrection) [S-03][S-11] | Lowest solo clear speed at Tier 2: "can't push content" [S-03]; slow early solo [S-11][S-13] |
| Always wanted in dungeons; rated mandatory for party content [S-13] | Erosion stacks slowly; summons underwhelm at lower tiers [S-11] ❓ |
| Extreme build flexibility from high base stats [S-03] | Still squishy; cannot tank [S-04] |
| Highest ceiling with investment: Arcanist and Dominator are both rated S [S-13] | A free-to-play Sage heals about 25% of an ally's HP per round; a heavy spender over 100% per skill [S-03] |

## Unlock and next class

- Unlock: Lv 44 at Cinder Ridge, from Mage (Mage class level 18) [S-03][S-10].
- Next: [Arcanist](arcanist.md) at Lv 82 in Aqualis (Sage class level 16 per [S-10][S-85]; level per [S-11][S-85]).
- Rolling back to Mage and promoting to Sage only (no Tier 3) is the way to aim Prayer pulls at Sage skills such as Resurrection [S-03].

## Builds

Fandom's class guide names three builds, and says "Healer" is a style (a stat direction), not a build [S-03]:

- **Erosion**: stack the Erosion debuff, then set it off for a large instant burst.
- **Direct damage**: plain Dark hits, similar to a battle-focused Duelist.
- **Summoner**: fight through summons. The Fandom author does not recommend focusing on it [S-03].

Skill line-ups below are from tier D guides [S-04][S-11] checked against the Fandom skill texts [S-03].

| Build | Techniques (cast order) | Charms | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Erosion (solo default) | Dark Bullet, Weakening Hex, Iron Thorn (or Flame Wolf Summon), Shadow Impact last | Shadow Erosion, Insight, Curse Resonance, Mana Surge (or Gale Shield) | Story, bosses. Shadow Impact triggers Erosion twice on targets that carry it |
| Dark hybrid with summons | Dark Bullet, Shadow Impact, Weakening Hex, Flame Wolf Summon | Shadow Erosion, Soul Impact, Insight, Gale Shield | Levelling when Erosion alone is too slow |
| Summoner | Flame Wolf Summon, Treantling Summon, Void Blessing, Radiant Restoration | Soul Impact, Mana Surge, Gale Shield, Elemental Body | Soul Impact turns every expiring summon into a Dark AoE. Skip Soul Spark with wolves: it cuts 2 turns from a 2-turn summon |
| Healer (party) | Healing Touch, Void Blessing, Radiant Restoration, Iron Thorn | Healing Mastery, Resurrection, Tough Soul, Gale Shield | Dungeons and raids |
| PvP support | Radiant Restoration, Healing Touch, Weakening Hex, Treantling Summon | Resurrection, Healing Mastery, Tough Soul, Gale Shield | 4v4; keep the team alive, weaken the enemy carry |

Build notes:
- **Erosion engine:** Dark Bullet has a 50% base chance to apply Erosion; Shadow Erosion adds a 40% base chance on every Dark technique hit; Insight raises Effect Hit Rate so those "base chances" land; Curse Resonance adds Technique damage per debuff on the target (up to 3) [S-03].
- **Resurrection** revives one ally once per battle with a heal and a 2-turn Revitalization buff; if several allies are already dead at battle start it picks the one with the highest Power [S-03].
- **Summons cannot be the primary target** of attacks or heals, so they are damage and body-blocking, not tanks (the Mage's Stonechief is the decoy) [S-03].
- Erosion and summoner builds become viable at Tier 2 but only compete with damage classes from Tier 3 [S-04] ❓.

## Relic element

Sage: **Dark**. Max Dark resonance first, then the other elements [S-01].

## Fantomon

- Support: main "your preference or a Support Fantomon"; Support aptitudes give DMG Boost and DMG Reduction. A damage Sage follows the DPS rule (Nyxarchon or an Attack type) [S-01].
- Season 2 order for tanks and healers: Mythic Aegiswing to Adult, Pandarial to Immortal (deployed), then Aegiswing to Immortal; a support pours aptitude resources into Pandarial for Healing Boost [S-02]. See [Fantomons](../systems/fantomons.md).

## Gems and companions

- Gems: Red/Purple, plus Orange (HP% + Healing Boost) or Green (HP%) [S-01]. See [Gems](../systems/gems.md).
- Companions: healers level by preference; Healing Boost only unlocks at companion Lv 100 [S-01]. Season 2: Suimo gives Healing Boost, Crit Res and DMG Res [S-02]. See [Companions](../systems/companions.md).

## When to move on

- Promote to Arcanist at Lv 82: that is where Erosion gets its finisher (Shadow of Termination), a cheat-death charm and real summons [S-11][S-04].
- A Sorcerer who wants to become a Sage should wait for the free lossless change at Lv 100 rather than re-pull [S-03].
