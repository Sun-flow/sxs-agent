# Berserker (Tier 3, Duelist line)

<!-- index: Read for the Berserker (Lv 82-106): what is known of the 3rd Duelist; reference only, our account is a Mage -->

> **Summary:** The Berserker is the "3rd Duelist": it keeps the burst and gains the AoE the Duelist lacked, and is rated S at Tier 3. Only four technique names are documented; builds and gear follow the Duelist page. It promotes to Conqueror at Lv 106.
> **Applies:** Lv 82-106 (Aqualis, end of Season 1)
> **Related:** [Duelist](duelist.md), [Tier 4: Conqueror](tier-4.md#conqueror-4th-duelist), [Classes overview](../systems/classes-overview.md)

## Role

- "The zenith of the Fighter's rage": burns its own life for bursts of power [S-03]. In-game tagline: let furious assaults be your shield [S-03].
- Gains strong AoE for level-appropriate content while keeping burst; relevant in PvP; rated S at Tier 3 [S-13][S-11].
- Base stat weighting: HP 1700, ATK 2400, DEF 1700, SPD 2400 [S-10]. Class stat: Crit Rate [S-10].
- A 3rd Duelist has four Legendary skills (2 Techniques, 2 Charms); one Legendary Charm is a core damage skill, which is why converting a Berserker into a Paladin is a poor trade [S-03].
- Fandom's Berserker page shows the Duelist tables unchanged [S-03].

## Unlock and next class

Lv 82 in Aqualis from Duelist [S-11][S-85] (Duelist class level 16 [S-10][S-85]); next Conqueror at Lv 106 [S-02].
