# Sorcerer (Tier 2, Mage line)

<!-- index: Read when playing or choosing the Sorcerer (Lv 44-82): skills, the five builds, stats, relic element, gear affixes, and when to move on to Archmage -->

> **Summary:** The Sorcerer is the Mage's pure-damage branch: the widest AoE and the steadiest hits in the game, and the most fragile body. It adds Light as a fourth element and has five builds (Light, Fire, Wind, Water, four-element hybrid). For PvE levelling run Fire or Light with Mana Surge and Void Bubble; keep Tier 1 staples such as Water Assault. It promotes to Archmage at Lv 82.
> **Applies:** Lv 44-82 (Cinder Ridge to Aqualis); Sorcerer skills stay usable on the line in all seasons
> **Related:** [Mage](mage.md), [Archmage](archmage.md), [Tier 4: Destroyer](tier-4.md#destroyer-4th-sorcerer), [Sage](sage.md), [Classes overview](../systems/classes-overview.md), [Skills](../systems/skills.md), [Relics](../systems/relics.md), [Gear](../systems/gear.md)

## Role

- "A master of elemental destruction": trades precision for scale, with large AoE spells [S-03]. Pure, raw firepower; hits hard and wide [S-03].
- Uses a staff with a Spellbook off-hand [S-11] ❓. Elements: Fire, Water, Wind and Light, everything except Dark [S-11][S-01].
- Class stat: Accuracy, so its attacks are rarely blocked. It gives up Crit for stability: damage per hit ranges 100% to 250%, less explosive than a Duelist (50% to 300%) but far more consistent [S-03]. A database site labels the class trait Block Penetration [S-10].
- Base stat weighting: HP 1000, ATK 1900, DEF 900, SPD 1200: highest ATK and lowest DEF of the Tier 2 classes [S-10].

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Best AoE and dungeon clear speed at Tier 2; rated S [S-13][S-11] | Most fragile of the four classes; "shatters the moment it gets touched" [S-03] |
| Consistent damage, low gear entry barrier, described as the safest long-term pick [S-11] ❓ | Weak 1v1 PvP and slow against single bosses until gear improves [S-11] ❓ |
| Five builds and four elements: always something the enemy is weak to [S-03] | Each element needs its own skills, relic resonance and loadout [S-03] |
| Good in group PvP [S-11] ❓ | Needs a frontline in hard party content [S-04] |

## Unlock and next class

- Unlock: Lv 44 at Cinder Ridge, from Mage (Mage class level 18) [S-03][S-10].

## Builds

The five build names and what each is for come from Fandom's class guide [S-03]. Skill line-ups are assembled from the skill texts [S-03] and tier D build guides [S-04][S-11]; treat exact slots as suggestions.

| Build | Idea [S-03] | Techniques (cast order) | Charms | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light | Solo fights; stacks hit count to trigger its charm damage. Mainstream | Energy Burst, Light of Dawn, Lightning Chain, Flickering Stars | Lightning Mystery, Mana Surge, Elemental Mystery, Void Bubble | Bosses, PvE and PvP alike [S-01] |
| Fire | Higher crit chance and the AoE advantage. Mainstream | Fiery Star Trail, Blazing Fire Ring, Fireball, Flame Jet (or Energy Burst first) | Heart of Flame, Raging Wildfire, Mana Surge, Void Bubble | PvE clearing; better in PvE than PvP [S-01] |
| Wind | Repels enemies so melee attackers lack the "proactiveness" (energy) to use skills | Cyclone, Wind's Delight, Tempest Sphere, Iron Thorn | Wind's Shadow, Unstable Aura, Wind's Whisper, Gale Shield | PvP [S-01] |
| Water ("Fridge") | Control: keeps enemies Frozen so they lose turns | Water Assault, Frosty Nova, Ice Spike, Water Shot | Water to Ice, Dew's Blessing, Mana Surge, Curse Resonance | 4v4 more than PvE [S-01]; boss lock [S-04] |
| Four-element hybrid | One damaging technique per element so Elemental Harmony reaches 4 stacks of Elemental Mastery | Lightning Chain, Fiery Star Trail, Wind's Delight, Frosty Nova (or Water Assault) | Elemental Harmony, Mana Surge, Unstable Aura, Lightning Mystery | General PvE without swapping loadouts [S-04] |

Build notes:
- **Energy Burst** makes the next damaging technique deal 50% more and adds Accuracy: place it directly before the hardest hit [S-03].
- **Elemental Harmony** counts elements among equipped *damaging* techniques, up to 4 [S-03]. The hybrid is the least gear-specific build but each element's relic resonance contributes less.
- **Raging Wildfire + Flaming Path** together waste a slot: a tile holds one burn [S-12] ❓.
- **Void Bubble** is the survival charm: a max-HP shield plus Dodge for 2 turns the first time HP falls below half [S-03]. Before owning it, keep Gale Shield.
- **Lightning Mystery** (30% extra Light hit per damaging technique hit) rewards many-hit skills: Lightning Chain (up to 10 targets), Tempest Sphere (5 hits), Wind's Delight (7 waves) [S-03].
- Dungeons: Fire or hybrid AoE. Solo bosses: Light. Arena: Wind or Light [S-01][S-03].

## Relic element

Sorcerer: Wind, Water, Fire or Light by preference. Wind: PvP. Water: better in 4v4 than PvE. Fire: better in PvE than PvP. Light: equally good in both. Max the chosen element's resonance first, then the others [S-01]. For this account (PvE, solo): Fire or Light.

## Fantomon

- Damage dealers: main Nyxarchon or an Attack-type Fantomon; Attack aptitudes give Crit Rate and Accuracy [S-01].
- Main is chosen for aptitudes, Deployed for its passive or active skill; one Fantomon of each type as subs [S-01].
- Season 2 order for DPS: Mythic Nyxarchon to Adult, then Pandarial to Immortal (deployed), then Nyxarchon to Immortal (about 30% Crit Rate and Accuracy), Aegiswing last [S-02]. See [Fantomons](../systems/fantomons.md).

## Top gear affixes

Keep or inherit pieces with these, in SOF's order [S-02]:

1. Crit Rate + Crit DMG
2. Speed / HP / Defense (converted) to Attack
3. Crit Rate + Accuracy
4. Flash (appears from Loong Haven)
5. Technique damage dealt to enemies with debuffs

One or two good affixes per piece is enough for a free-to-play account [S-02]. See [Gear](../systems/gear.md).

## Gems and companions

- Gems: Red/Purple (ATK%, SPD% + Crit Rate), Yellow (SPD%), Green (HP%) [S-01]. See [Gems](../systems/gems.md).
- Companions: every companion to Lv 20, then the class-matching ones to 50. DPS wants Crit Rate and Accuracy at companion Lv 50 [S-01]. Season 2 companions: Wuji and Ming (Crit Rate, DMG Boost), Jiangwang (Accuracy, DMG Boost) [S-02]. See [Companions](../systems/companions.md).

## When to move on

- Promote to Archmage as soon as Lv 82 and the class-level gate are met; nothing is lost by promoting, and Sorcerer skills stay usable [S-03].
- Consider the Sage line instead only at Lv 100+, with the free lossless change, if the account will mostly play party content as a healer [S-03].
