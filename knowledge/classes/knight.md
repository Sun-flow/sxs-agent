# Knight (Tier 2, Warrior line)

<!-- index: Read for the Knight (Lv 44-82): skills, Block / Water / Light builds, stats, relic element and gear affixes; reference for party play and class change -->

> **Summary:** The Knight is the tank: Block, taunt and shields, with counter-damage based on DEF. Every dungeon party wants one, but it is the slowest solo class and the hardest to play free-to-play. Builds: Block (sustain), Water (AoE through shield breaks and Cold) and Light. Water or Light relics, Aegiswing or a Tank Fantomon, Block + Block Efficiency gear. It promotes to Paladin at Lv 82.
> **Applies:** Lv 44-82; Knight skills stay usable on the line in all seasons
> **Related:** [Warrior](warrior.md), [Paladin](paladin.md), [Tier 4: Guardian](tier-4.md#guardian-4th-knight), [Classes overview](../systems/classes-overview.md), [Dungeons](../systems/dungeons.md)

## Role

- "An unyielding monument": plate and a great shield, there to block for allies [S-03]. Elements: Physical, Light, Water; Shield off-hand [S-11] ❓.
- Class stat: Block. High Block allows sustained fights; a little Crit gives strong hits when luck aligns; damage 50% to 200% per hit [S-03].
- Block mechanics: a blocked hit deals 50% of the skill's base damage and ignores the crit bonus; enemy Accuracy subtracts from Block (20% Accuracy needs 120% Block for a full block) [S-11] ❓.
- Base stat weighting: HP 1500, ATK 900, DEF 1500, SPD 1100 [S-10].

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Needed in dungeons and raids; a strong Knight makes every damage dealer comfortable [S-03][S-13] | Lowest damage; slow solo story [S-13] |
| Taunt (Heart of Challenge) and stuns [S-03] | Rated B in every tier list [S-13][S-11] |
| Can later go pure damage or full sustain [S-03] | Least free-to-play friendly: endgame needs heavy Block investment [S-11] ❓ |

## Unlock and next class

- Lv 44 at Cinder Ridge from Warrior [S-03]. Next: [Paladin](paladin.md) at Lv 82 [S-11][S-85].

## Builds

Fandom names three builds [S-03]: **Block Knight** (sustain for long fights; all Knights need Block, only this build is called Block), **Water Knight** (Water skills, strong AoE) and **Light Knight** (Light attacks). Styles: Tank is standard; "Crit Knight" and "Heal Knight" (healing boost instead of accuracy/crit as secondary stats) are rare. Mechanics: Shield, Taunt [S-03].

| Build | Techniques | Charms | Use | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dungeon tank | Valor Surge, Heart of Challenge, Luminous Shield, Guardian Ring | Iron Fortress, Block Awareness, Rebound, Ripple Impact | Party tanking | [S-11] ❓ |
| Defensive counter | Heart of Challenge, Ricocheting Shield, Valor Surge, Guardian Ring | Rebound, Eye for an Eye, Defensive Assault, Ripple Impact | Solo and PvP | [S-04][S-11] ❓ |
| Water / shield-break | Guardian Ring, Frostbite Blossom, Ricocheting Shield, Heart of Challenge | Ripple Impact, Defensive Assault, Pure Protection, Rebound | AoE: every broken shield is a Water AoE with 3 Cold stacks | [S-03] texts; line-up ❓ |

- Valor Surge and Heart of Challenge cast once before / at battle start, so the party opens with a damage buff and the enemies taunted [S-03].
- Rebound and Guardian Ring scale with DEF; Defensive Assault gives ATK while shielded [S-03].
