# Towers

<!-- index: Read before climbing Fantasia Ascent or Demonbind Tower, or when looking for ore, tools or gems from tower floors -->

> **Summary:** Fantasia Ascent is the solo tower: one floor per fight, permanent progress, first-clear rewards of ore and tools, and an Auto Challenge mode that keeps climbing in the background. Climb every day as far as power allows and never retry a loss. Demonbind Tower is Loong Haven's tower, opens at level 116 and is the tower that drops gems.
> **Applies:** all seasons (Demonbind Tower: Season 2+)
> **Related:** [Dungeons](dungeons.md), [Gems](gems.md), [Gear](gear.md), [Material Realms](material-realms.md), [Monsters](monsters.md), [Levels and unlocks](levels-and-unlocks.md), rules R-079, R-094, R-112, R-127, R-128

## Fantasia Ascent

### Reaching it

- Journal (home bottom bar, second icon), card Fantasia Ascent [R-094]; or the quest "Into the Moon's Shadow I" Go button [R-079].
- The Journal itself unlocks when any mode is unlocked [R-011].

### How a climb works

| Step | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Floor list | F1 at the bottom; each floor shows a recommended power and a Challenge button; a Goals tab pays milestone rewards | [R-079] |
| Fight | Automatic, up to 15 rounds | [R-079] |
| Victory | Clear Rewards (ores, Worn Knuckles), Continue goes to the next floor | [R-079] |
| Defeat | A Retry button appears; progress is kept at the last cleared floor | [R-079] |
| Auto Challenge | Unlocks after floor 3 is cleared; a checkbox on the result screen; with it on, Continue counts down 3 s and the climb goes on by itself | [R-079] |
| Background climb | With Auto Challenge on, the tower keeps climbing after you leave the screen (we went F6 to F24 in one evening); the floor list shows "Auto-Challenging" and Resume | [R-079] [R-112] |
| Difficulty | Floors are gated by CP: the recommended power rises with each floor | [R-079] [S-45] |

- A loss switches Auto Challenge off [R-079].
- Tower fights can run minimised like Conquest fights, so the climb costs no play time [R-112] [S-04].

### Rewards and why it matters

- Fantasia Ascent is described as the top free ore source early on: higher floors pay more ore and better rarities [S-45]. Refined Ore comes from here and from dismantling gear [R-079].
- Worn Knuckles (the tool for the Dread Hollow realm, which gives Battle Essence) drop as clear rewards [R-079]. See [Material Realms](material-realms.md).
- "Fantasia Ascent once" is one of the six Daily Quests, so at least one challenge a day is needed for the daily set [R-128].
- Pushing floors is one of the things better dungeon gear unlocks: more CP means more floors [S-01].
- Floor rewards are first-clear rewards; no source describes a repeatable daily sweep ❓.

### What we do (bot rules)

- One climb a day with Auto Challenge on; never press Retry after a defeat [R-079].
- Challenge a floor only when its recommended power is within 1.25x of ours [R-127].

## Demonbind Tower (Season 2)

- Opens at level 116, which comes after the Tier 4 class change (Lv 106) and Adult Fantomon evolution (Lv 108) [S-02].
- It is a gem source: Season 2 adds gem farming here and in the open world (gems appear on the map like ingredients) [S-02].
- Gems are front-loaded power and fall in price over a season; do not over-invest, but take the free gems the tower gives [S-02]. See [Gems](gems.md).
- An Astral Pact bonus at 800 Primostars (F2P target) gives Gem Drop +5% [S-02]. See [Astral Pact](astral-pact.md).
- ❓ Floor count, attempts, whether it has Auto Challenge, and the gem tiers per floor are not in any source we have.
