# Cart and home

<!-- index: Read when upgrading the Cart, collecting AFK income or Bed EXP, using speed-ups, or deciding what Wood and Stone are for -->

> **Summary:** The Cart makes every basic material while you are away (36-hour cap); the Bed makes EXP. Raise all Cart rows to Lv 10, then put Wood and Stone first (Stone slightly ahead), then Rolla, then Ore and Sand, then Battle Essence, then Treats. Lost Goddess Statues raise Blessing Level, which caps Cart rows and lifts Bed EXP, so activate statues before using speed-ups. Keep the same Cart plan every season.
> **Applies:** all seasons

## The Cart

### Production rows

| Row | Unlock | Used for | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rolla | start | everything | [R-020] |
| Wood | start | Cart upgrades | [R-020] |
| Stone | start | Cart upgrades | [R-020] |
| Raw Ore | early (guide sites say Cart Lv 3 ❓) | gear | [R-020][S-35] |
| Battle Essence | early (Cart Lv 3 ❓) | skills | [R-020][S-35] |
| Gear | Lv 15 in our game; guide sites say Lv 10 ❓ | free gear drops | [R-020][S-35] |
| Chrono Sand | press the free Unlock button when it appears | relics | [R-137] |
| Treats | Lv 45 | Fantomon EXP | [R-020] |

- The Cart "generates resources while idle. Upgrade each resource to increase production and storage" [S-03].
- **Idle rewards cap at 36 hours**: claim regularly [S-03].
- Activating Goddess Statues and the Monthly Pass boost production [S-03].
- A row's level is capped by Blessing Level: at the cap the button reads "Unlocks at Blessing Lv. N" [R-136][S-03].
- Upgrade arrows show only after the Cart has been claimed, so claim first [R-136].
- A speed-up or Temporal Crystal also gives the Cart 2 hours of production [S-03].

### Upgrade cost

Each row upgrade costs ordinary resources only: the other building material, the row's own material, and Rolla. For
Stone and Wood at Lv 3 to 4: 65 + 25 + 190; Lv 4 to 5: 70 + 30 + 205; Lv 5 to 6: 75 + 30 + 215 [R-136]. Later
costs are not recorded ❓.

- SOF's alternative order: Wood and Stone, then Ore and Sand, then Rolla, then Battle Essence, then Treats [S-01].
  One guide site puts Sand and Ore second and makes Rolla conditional on running three or more shop refreshes a day,
  with focus rows kept about 20 to 30% above the rest ❓ [S-32].
- Our bot today upgrades only Stone and Wood, evenly, Stone first on a tie [R-136]. That matches step 1 but not
  step 0 or steps 2 to 5; see [conflicts.md](../conflicts.md).

### Why Wood and Stone first

Goal: the highest long-term average income from the Cart [S-01].

- Cost: Wood and Stone give no power themselves, so early-season power is lower, most of all in Season 1 [S-01].
- Gain: the Cart levels faster; you reach storage overflow earlier; and you enter the next season with a higher
  Cart than players who chose otherwise [S-01].
- Requirement: be consistent every season. Skipping one season can lose most of the advantage [S-01].
- At a new season the Cart's cap rises with the new statues, and the Wood and Stone stored during the old season
  pay for the first new levels at once [S-02].

"Storage overflow" is not defined in the guide. The likely meaning: once the Cart is at its cap for the season,
Wood and Stone keep piling up as a stockpile for the next cap ❓.

### Cart gear drops

- The Gear row drops gear for free; the higher the Cart, the better the chance of high-tier Legendary gear [S-01].
- When a new dungeon opens, the Cart can at once drop Legendary gear above your current Mythic pieces: SOF got a
  piece 15K CP above his Mythic gear on the day Aquorigin opened, and entered with two Cart pieces equipped [S-01].
- So early in a new season the first dungeon's shards and gear can be sold: by the next dungeon the Cart should
  already drop stronger Legendary gear [S-02]. See [Auction house](auction-house.md).

### The Rolla strategy

A second school: make as much Rolla as possible (Rolla realm runs, Rolla Cart row, even stamina) to refresh the
Vendor Booth often and buy Wood and Stone there [S-01].

| | |
|---|---|
| For | refreshes also bring extra Stellatie, Bond Trinkets and other items, which adds up [S-01] |
| Against | Wood and Stone per refresh are limited; overall Cart level ends lower; more effort to balance; a lower Cart means worse gear drops [S-01] |
| Verdict | valid, but SOF does not think it worthwhile [S-01]; still "viable" in Season 2 [S-02] |

## The Bed

- Sleeping earns AFK EXP into an EXP Reserve; the Bed screen shows Blessing Level and EXP per hour [R-035].
- Collect when the blue orbs and the red "!" show; one collection can give several levels [R-101]. At the level
  cap the EXP banks until Rank Advancement [R-118].
- It has an Auto Collect switch [S-02] (one site calls it Quick Claim ❓ [S-34]).
- The Bed cannot be upgraded with Rolla or Dawnium: only Lost Goddess Statues raise it ❓ [S-35]. Each zone statue
  adds one Blessing Level, e.g. Blessing 5 to 6 raised EXP per hour 592 to 608 and the Cart cap 5 to 6 [R-035].

### Speed-ups

- One free speed-up a day; a Temporal Crystal does the same: 2 hours of income for both Bed and Cart [S-03].
- **Activate every available Goddess Statue before using a speed-up**, so the 2 hours are paid at the higher rate
  [S-03]. Our rule saves the free speed-up for after the day's exploration [R-035].

### Banking before a new season

| When | Do | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 36 hours before the new kingdom opens | stop collecting Bed EXP; turn Auto Collect off | [S-02] |
| On entering the new kingdom | claim the stored EXP | [S-02] |
| First days | do not use the free speed-up or Temporal Crystals until the new statues are active | [S-02] |

The 36 hours match the idle cap [S-03]. See [Seasons](seasons.md).

## Other free income on Home

- Mailbox: Claim All daily; never Delete Read [R-168].
