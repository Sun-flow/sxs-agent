# Goddess Statues

<!-- index: Read before activating statues, using a bed speed-up or Temporal Crystal, or choosing where to spend a kingdom tool -->

> **Summary:** Every zone has a statue that can be lit once the zone is 100% explored. Big Goddess Statues (main
> zones) raise Blessing Level: +1 Cart level cap and more bed EXP per hour. Small Lost Goddess Statues (side zones,
> many behind kingdom tools) give +1% Home EXP and +1% of one Cart resource. Light every statue you can before
> using any speed-up, and spend tools where the most statues sit.
> **Applies:** all seasons
> **Related:** [Exploration](exploration.md), [Cart and home](cart-and-home.md),
> [Levels and unlocks](levels-and-unlocks.md), [World and kingdoms](world-and-kingdoms.md); rules R-070, R-035,
> R-101, R-135, R-148, R-151, R-161

## Two kinds of statue

| | Goddess Statue | Lost Goddess Statue |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Main (numbered, story) zones | Side zones, often behind a gate or a kingdom tool |
| Requirement | 100% exploration of its zone | same |
| Cost | Free "Unlock" | Free "Unlock" |
| Effect | Blessing Level +1: Max Cart Level +1 and higher base bed EXP per hour | Home EXP Yield +1% and +1% yield of one home resource (Wood, Stone, Rolla, Raw Ore ...) |
| Map icon | Gold statue; red "!" when ready | Orange trident-like icon in the legend ("Lost Statue"); blue "!" when ready |
| Count in Verdantglade | 17 | 49 |
| Count in later kingdoms | 18 each | Cinder Ridge 68, Aqualis 74, Loong Haven 79, Aethyris 78, Harpadia 82, Ignis 81 |
| Sources | [R-070][S-03] | [R-070][S-03]; counts [S-50][S-85] |

- The official tip text: main story statues raise Blessing Level, which raises Cart resource level caps and base
  bed EXP; Lost Goddess Statues "also boost resource and EXP gains" [S-03].
- A lit statue's card only shows "Regional Relics" and no button [R-070].
- Hero and Witch statues are a different thing. A Hero Statue is a "Statue of <name>" card: Visit gives a
  permanent stat boost and a full heal, and the card shows daily visit attempts [R-144]. A Witch Statue is a
  Blight Witch object: a lore page, then a curse for one battle [R-174]. The official checklist says each can be
  used once a day for Stellatie or stamina [S-40] ❓; the rules are tier A and describe what we saw. Verdantglade
  has 4 Hero Statues (Hidden Camp, Guardian Vale, Hurricane Heights, Verdantglade XVII) and 2 Witch Statues
  (Bubble Lake, Spider Queen Lair) [S-50][S-85]. See [Exploration](exploration.md).

## What Blessing Level does

- It caps the level of every Cart production row (Blessing 7 lets Wood and Stone reach Lv 7) [R-035][R-136].
- It raises bed EXP per hour. Observed steps on our account: level 2 took AFK EXP from 50 to 500 per hour;
  5 to 6 gave 592 to 608; 6 to 7 gave 620 to 636; 7 to 8 gave 636 to 657 [R-070].
- The publisher: Cart resource level caps depend on Blessing Level, and Goddess Statues and the Monthly Pass
  raise production [S-87].
- With 17 Goddess Statues in Verdantglade alone [S-50][S-85], Blessing Level should be near 18-19 by the end of that
  kingdom ❓ (Originisle gives the first step).

## What the passive EXP does

- The bed stores EXP while you are away; idle rewards cap at 36 hours [S-03][S-87]. Collecting can give several levels
  at once [R-101].
- Bed EXP banked while the character is at a level cap is applied the moment the rank advances (Lv 30 to 37 in
  one step on our account) [R-118].
- Bed EXP is the main source of levels, so statues decide how fast every level-gated feature opens
  ([Levels and unlocks](levels-and-unlocks.md)).

## Speed-ups and Temporal Crystals

- The bed has one free speed-up a day [R-035][S-03][S-87].
- A Temporal Crystal gives 2 hours of production for both the bed and the Cart [S-03][S-87].
- Both pay out at the current rates, so **light every available statue first** [S-03][S-87]. In a new kingdom do not use
  the free speed-up or any Temporal Crystal until the new statues are lit: "these will give you a huge exp boost"
  [S-02]. Our rule already holds the free speed-up until the day's exploration is done [R-035].
- On a season's launch morning: claim season rewards, spend Primostars, push the map, and only then use speed-ups
  and Temporal Crystals [S-53].
- Before a new season: stop collecting bed EXP 36 hours before launch (turn off Auto Collect) so a full 36 h
  reserve is claimed inside the new kingdom [S-02][S-53]. See [Seasons](seasons.md).
- Alternative for late season: keep speed-ups for the end of the season to push character level for Primostars
  [S-53].

## Statues behind kingdom tools

- Every tool-gated area holds at least one statue, which is why tools are the first Kingdom Shop purchase [S-01].
- Verdantglade has 11 tool-gated spots: 5 Withering Potions, 3 Magic Drills, 3 Gateway Keys [S-50][S-85].

| Tool | Verdantglade spots (nearest named area on eog's map; approximate) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Gateway Key (3) | Rabbit Vault, Gorge Depths, Owl Vault | [S-85] |
| Withering Potion (5) | Jungle Vault, Monolith Ruins V, Stagnant Jungle, Sunken Forest, Sunrise Isle | [S-85] |
| Magic Drill (3) | Vine Peninsula, Hidden Camp, Hideout | [S-85] |

- Tools needed in later kingdoms (Key / Potion / Drill / Water Mine): Cinder Ridge 4 / 3 / 5 / 3, Aqualis
  5 / 2 / 3 / 6, Loong Haven 5 / 1 / 1 / 2 plus 7 Cloud Jade [S-85]. Spot names per kingdom are in the
  [eog notes](../sources/notes/eog.md); every map point is in [data/map-points.json](../data/map-points.json).
- **Best single use in Verdantglade:** one Magic Drill opens an area with **two** Lost Goddess Statues. The guide's
  map marks the drill spot at the south-west corner of Mushroom Woods, with Mushroom Cluster inside the same
  boxed area; it lies south of Guardian Vale and north of Vine Peninsula. "This is your highest drill priority"
  [S-01]. eog's nearest area name for a drill spot in that part of the map is Vine Peninsula [S-85]; that the
  two are the same spot is our inference ❓.
- Hidden Camp is another drill spot: a Boulder Blockade with pickups and a Lost Goddess Statue behind it
  [R-151][S-85]. The third is Hideout [S-85].
- The Magic Drill is a Cinder Ridge item, so these Verdantglade statues are picked up on a return trip [S-01].

## Practical order

1. Reach 100% in the zone ([Exploration](exploration.md)); the statue shows a "!" on the area map [R-148].
2. Unlock it at once; it is free and permanent.
3. Check the Cart: a new Blessing Level allows one more level on each row [R-136].
4. Only then use the daily speed-up or Temporal Crystals.
