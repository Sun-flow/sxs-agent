# Gift codes

<!-- index: Read when redeeming gift codes or looking for free Dawnium, badges and Stellatie from codes -->

> **Summary:** Redeem at Home > Main Menu (bottom right) > User Center > Gift Code; rewards arrive in the Mailbox (the owl on the house). Every code below is unverified and may be expired: try each once and record the result.
> **Applies:** all seasons
> **Related:** [Currencies](currencies.md), [Cart and home](cart-and-home.md), rules R-168, R-043

## How to redeem

1. Home > Main Menu (bottom-right corner) > User Center > Gift Code [S-03][S-85][S-80].
2. Type the code exactly (codes are case-sensitive capitals) and confirm [S-80][S-04].
3. Collect from the Mailbox: the owl on the house's left balcony, Claim All [R-168].

On iOS, codes are entered on a web page after linking the account, not in the app [S-37]. Our bot runs the Android
build, so the in-game route applies. Redeeming is free; "Link Account" prompts elsewhere stay off limits for the bot
[R-096].
