# Auction house and player market (Commercium)

<!-- index: Read when selling shards, gear, gems or recipes to other players for Dawnium, or before buying anything from the market -->

> **Summary:** The Commercium (airship from Home, Lv 40) has a Trading House and an Auction House. F2P players fund their Dawnium spending by selling: extra dungeon shards and gear, white and black gems, recipes. Sell shards before the season rolls over. Buy little: watch for gear whose seller missed a valuable affix, and wait for the gem price crash before buying gems.
> **Applies:** character Lv 40+; affix trading from Season 2
> **Related:** [Currencies](currencies.md#dawnium-income-and-spending), [Gear](gear.md), [Gems](gems.md), [Dungeons](dungeons.md), [Seasons](seasons.md), [Cooking and dishes](cooking-and-dishes.md), rules R-043, R-166

## Access

- At character Lv 40, board the airship at the western edge of Home to reach the Commercium [S-03]. Our Home shows
  the Airship with "Unlocks at Lv. 40" [R-166].
- It holds a **Trading House** and an **Auction House** for buying and selling gear and recipes [S-03].
- Hermes, the Verdantglade merchant, is later met in the Commercium [S-03].

## The two halls

| | Trading House | Auction House |
|---|---|---|
| Goods | gear and recipes [S-03]; common materials and upgrade items ❓ [S-39] | gear and recipes; "premium gear" [S-03]; permanent-stat food and its recipes [S-31] |
| Price rules | stricter price controls ❓ [S-39] | open bidding ❓ [S-39] |
| Currency | Dawnium ❓ | bidding on premium gear needs **Luminium** (the paid currency) [S-03] |

- Luminium converts 1:1 into Dawnium [S-35][S-38], so a sale paid in Luminium is as good as Dawnium to the seller ❓
  (which currency a seller receives is not stated in any source).
- Gems bought from "the trading house" are excluded from the Primal Gem relic's bonus, which confirms gems trade
  there [S-33].

## How F2P earns Dawnium by selling

1. Wear drop-bonus gear: pieces with the highlighted dungeon-reward stats, plus the Lucky Statue relic in the main
   relic line. The gear is region specific (Aqualis pieces work only in Aqualis dungeons) [S-01].
2. Enter the highest dungeon you can get into and ask for a carry; you only need to meet the entry threshold
   [S-01].
3. The setup raises the chance to double shard and gear drops and to upgrade a drop's rarity (Legendary to Mythic)
   [S-01].
4. Gear yourself first; after that keep running the same way and sell the extra shards [S-01].
5. Reinvest the Dawnium (Material Realm tools, relic gacha) [S-01].

At the start of a season there are two schools for the first dungeon's Mythic gear: sell shards for a guaranteed
profit, or keep rolling for a valuable affix; either way make the next dungeon's CP threshold first [S-02]. The Cart
soon drops Legendary gear stronger than the first dungeon's Mythic gear, which is why selling is safe [S-02].

## Timing

| When | Do | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before the season ends | sell spare shards | they lose their sale value at the rollover ❓ | [S-34] |
| Last week of a season | buy cheap Aqua-tier Divine shards or gear if you need rating points | old-dungeon gear gets cheap and still counts for the rating | [S-02] |
| Start of a season, new dungeon | sell first-dungeon shards and gear early | prices are highest while few have them | [S-02] |
| When gem prices crash | buy higher-tier gems | gems are a small share of power, demand falls as players saturate | [S-02] |
| Right after a launch or patch | avoid buying | prices are inflated ❓ | [S-39] |

SOF cannot say when the gem crash comes, only to wait for it before buying [S-02].

## Pricing advice

- Price by affix, not by rarity colour. Look for gear "mispriced due to the seller failing to recognize the value
  of the affix(es)" [S-02], and do not make that mistake when selling. Valuable affixes per class are listed in
  [Gear](gear.md): for the Mage line, Crit Rate + Crit DMG, stats converted to Attack, Crit Rate + Accuracy, Flash
  (from Loong Haven), technique damage against debuffed enemies [S-02].
- Affixes on first-dungeon gear still matter: once inherited onto seasonal gear they get the seasonal bonus [S-02].
- Check current listings before setting a price ❓ [S-39].
- Buying is optional and never a daily need; bid only for an item that fixes a current bottleneck ❓ [S-30][S-35].

## Fees and limits

No source gives the sales tax, listing fee, number of listing slots or listing duration ❓. Read them from the
listing screen before the first sale.

## Bot policy

Selling and buying on the market are spending-type actions: the bot does neither without an explicit rule from mae
[R-043].
