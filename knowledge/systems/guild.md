# Guild

<!-- index: Read before any guild decision: joining, how many donations to buy, where to AFK, Conquest and Guild Clash, and what to buy in the Guild Shop each week -->

> **Summary:** Join an active guild as soon as the feature unlocks (Lv 15 by the game's text). Every day: donate (first is free, guides recommend two paid ones for 70 Dawnium in total), keep the character AFK in a guild facility, and use both Conquest attempts in the background. Every week: spend Guild Tokens on Fantomon aptitude materials and summon tokens first. Guild buffs are permanent power, and guildmates are the source of dungeon carries.
> **Applies:** all seasons
> **Related:** [Fantomons](fantomons.md), [Shops](shops.md), [Currencies](currencies.md), [Dungeons](dungeons.md), [Events](events.md), [Arena and PvP](arena-and-pvp.md), [Monsters](monsters.md), [Seasons](seasons.md), rules R-102, R-103, R-110, R-111, R-112, R-113, R-114

## Daily guild routine

### Donation

| Donation | Cost | Reward | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | Free | 300 Guild Tokens + 500 of each guild resource | [R-103] |
| 2nd | 20 Dawnium | same | [R-103] |
| 3rd | 50 Dawnium | same | [R-103] |
| 4th, 5th | ❓ higher | same | [S-03] |
| Daily cap | 5 | | [R-103] [S-03] |

- The official handbook says spending 70 Dawnium for two additional attempts is "highly recommended for progression" [S-03]. That matches our observed prices exactly: 20 + 50 = 70 [R-103].
- Community advice: three donations a day (1 free + 2 paid) for free players, five for spenders [S-44] [S-45].
- Our bot's binding rule is tighter: two donations a day, never pay above 20 Dawnium [R-103]. This is a difference from the guides; see [conflicts](../conflicts.md).

### AFK gathering

- Put the character in one facility slot: +600 per hour to the guild, capped at 36 hours; personally 120 Guild Tokens per hour plus a random item [R-110].
- One slot per character; switching facility collects the old slot. Our rule keeps the character at the Forge [R-110].
- Collect and restart before the 36-hour cap. Fill empty slots: facility output is the guild's building income ❓ [S-04].

### Conquest (guild boss)

- At the Gate of Expedition. A shared boss per floor; 2 challenge attempts a day; 50-round automatic fight that can run in the background [R-111] [R-112].
- Rewards per attempt: damage and participation rewards (10 Dawnium, 150 Guild Tokens). Beaten floors have a chest to claim. Goals tab: Serverwide (per server floor) and Personal (total challenges 10, 20, ...) [R-111].
- "Conquest once" is one of the six Daily Quests [R-128].
- How far the guild climbs depends on its strongest members; guild bosses are said to drop Skill Shard Vouchers, so never skip them ❓ [S-44].
