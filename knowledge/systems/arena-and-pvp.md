# Arena and PvP

<!-- index: Read before spending Arena Tickets, when planning the weekly Arena climb, or when choosing skills, relics and Fantomons for PvP and 4v4 -->

> **Summary:** The Grand Arena (Cinder Ridge, Lv 40 plus Rank Advancement) is ticket-based PvP against other players' teams with a weekly ladder. Save tickets at the end of the week and spend them right after the weekly reset, when the ladder is full of bots, to sit high and collect weekly points for longer. Arena coins buy Fantomon shards, Stellatie and Covenite in the Arena Shop.
> **Applies:** all seasons, from Cinder Ridge on
> **Related:** [Shops](shops.md), [Currencies](currencies.md), [Fantomons](fantomons.md), [Relics](relics.md), [Classes overview](classes-overview.md), [Events](events.md), [Kingdom rank](kingdom-rank.md), [Levels and unlocks](levels-and-unlocks.md), rules R-057, R-024

- The two price ladders disagree; both start at 10 Dawnium and rise per fight within a day [S-42] [S-45]. Read the real price on the buy dialog.
- F2P advice on extra fights: about 2 extra fights a day is the cheap part of the ladder; it ranks below dungeon attempts, Material Realm tools and shop refreshes in Dawnium priority ❓ [S-45].

### The weekly reset trick

1. Toward the end of each Arena week, stop using tickets and let them pile up [S-01] [S-40].
2. Right after the weekly reset the ladder is filled with bots. Spend every saved ticket (and optionally buy more) at once, before strong players are awake; reaching rank 1 on the spot is possible [S-01].
3. Even if you cannot hold the rank, the time spent high up gives a big head start in weekly Arena points [S-01]. Early-week rank also pays a daily Dawnium income for the rest of the week ❓ [S-43].
4. After the first day the ladder sorts itself by power, so later tickets move you little; buying more tickets later in the week is poor value ❓ [S-43].

- The official checklist gives the same tip: save tickets before the weekly reset and use them early in the new Arena season [S-40].
- The reset weekday is not confirmed ❓. Weekly achievement quests reset Monday 8:00 server time [R-024], and a fan site says the Arena week resets on the same clock as the daily reset [S-44].

### Points, rankings and rewards

- Rank gives weekly Arena points; a higher position for longer means more points [S-01].
- Rankings exist per mode: the Arena ladder, the server Power ranking (Top 100 list), dungeon and Material Realm ranking tabs, Conquest rankings and the Chaos Invasion class ranking [S-01] [R-095] [R-111] [S-03].
- ❓ The exact point table, weekly reward tiers and what the points buy are not in any source we have.

## Class and build notes for PvP

| Topic | Note | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sorcerer relic element | Wind is the PvP element; Water is better in 4v4 than in PvE; Fire is better in PvE than PvP; Light is equally good in both | [S-01] |
| Knight relic element | Water for AoE, Light otherwise | [S-01] |
| Duelist / Sage relic element | Duelist Fire, Sage Dark | [S-01] |
| Fantomon for PvP | Aegiswing has PvP utility; Pandarial (Season 2, day 46) is much better in 4v4 | [S-02] |
| Kels | A dispel Fantomon, used in Arena setups ❓ | [S-43] [S-04] |
| Stellaris relics | Paid-points relics give PvP Bonus DMG +4.8% and PvP DMG RES +4.8%; not available to F2P | [S-42] |
| Crit vs block | A crit deals 150% damage by default, reduced to 50% when the target blocks, so Block and Crit Res matter for PvP tanks ❓ | [S-49] |
| Elements | Fire is weak to Water, Dark is weak to Light, Wind is effective against Water; a unit is immune to its own element | [S-42] |
| Power | After the first day the ladder is ordered by power: CP decides most fights | [S-43] |

- Mage line: a Sorcerer who wants Arena results levels Wind resonance first; a Sage levels Dark [S-01]. See [Sorcerer](../classes/sorcerer.md), [Sage](../classes/sage.md) and [Mage](../classes/mage.md).
- Skill loadouts are edited at Home, and there are up to seven loadout slots, so keep a separate PvP loadout ❓ [S-42] [S-49].
