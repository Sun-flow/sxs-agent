> about one year and six seasons ahead. So far the global game has copied it exactly: the same server-day calendar
> (Season 1 ends day 46, Season 2 runs days 47-113, Season 3 starts day 114) and the same dungeon power thresholds

> ice-and-fire kingdom (tier 7) from about day 344, and an eighth tier from about day 428. Formation Breaker
> (Sorcerer) and Soul Pact Resonance (Sage) are the charms that stay useful longest; Pandarial becomes the best
> Fantomon for every class. **Everything here is a forecast (tier C at best): the global game can change numbers,

Notes:

- **What a cap means.** Each season has a base level and a "rank cap". Progress above the cap is season level and
  returns to the cap at season end; five things can exceed it: character level, gear enhancement, skill level,
  Fantomon level and relic level. S1 cap 100 (relic +10), S2 cap 130 (relic +13), "and so on" [S-92]. The S3-S5
  values above are our inference from that rule plus: the second S3 dungeon needs Lv 160 and the third gem socket
  needs average enhancement 160 [S-91]; S4 enhancement goes 160 to 190 and relic resonance 16 to 19 in three rank
  steps, and the second S4 dungeon needs Lv 190 [S-96][S-91]; at the start of S5 "the highest level before the
  season" for enhancement is 220 [S-96].
- **Rank bands.** Stamina gathering, Material Realm yield, friendship-point exchange and Destiny Fruit yield all
  grow with rank, and Hapadi has three rank steps (lower, middle, upper) [S-96]. The Ignis rank is 天使; guild
  boss floors from 211 and Bond Adventure monsters were raised to that rank [S-97].
- **Server merges.** Each season doubles the server group: 2 into 1 at S1, 4 into 1 at S2, 8 into 1 at S3; S4 was
  the last merge. Auction prices settle toward the more expensive group after a merge [S-92][S-96].
- **Countdown.** The season page shows a countdown from 7 days before the next season [S-92].
- **Season 1 naming.** CN counts the forest and mountain period as "before S1"; global counts it inside Season 1.

General rules from CN veterans:

- A higher tier's skills beat lower-tier skills on base value and multiplier, so class tier matters more than
  skill rarity; rarity only decides top-difficulty content [S-92].
- The class-level requirement for tier 4 is about 2.1 times that of tier 3; stage progress and daily check-ins are
  just enough to reach each change without extra pulls [S-92]. Low spenders: 50-55 class levels per tier and save
  the rest [S-95].
- The free weekly class change stays, and changing class re-rolls the skill investment ("dual class change"); many
  CN players swap class by tier [S-92]. A zero-spend route one veteran gives: tier 2 Duelist, 3 Sorcerer,
  4 Duelist, 5 Sage, 6 Sorcerer, 7 Sorcerer, 8 undecided; Knight and Sage only after tier 7 [S-92].
- State of the classes at tier 7 (test server): dungeon tank = Knight; main damage = Sorcerer, then Duelist; Sage
  best as healer. Ranking damage (top-5 averages): Sorcerer about 1300, Duelist 1000, Sage 600, Knight 270 (in
  units of 100M). Adventure-commission top 50: 28 Sorcerers, 11 Duelists, 8 Knights, 3 Sages [S-68].

## Relics

- Each kingdom has four Legendary 4-piece sets (Light, Dark, Wind, Water) plus pools by phase: Loong Haven two
  pools, feather kingdom three, Hapadi three, Ignis three [S-90][S-91]. Set names per kingdom are in the
  [CN notes](../sources/notes/cn-version.md).
- F2P rule that does not change: never buy relic pulls with Dawnium; finish the Rare (blue) set of a kingdom, then
  move on; use Destiny Fruit (命运果实) only to fetch missing relics, and hold it until the new kingdom's pool has
  been pulled and your rank is at the top step [S-92][S-96].
- Crit targets: bosses have about 20% block in Loong Haven (aim for 120% crit) and about 30% in the feather
  kingdom (130%); CN first needed 140% in the feather kingdom until the 2025-11-20 patch cut it to about 118% [S-92].
- Unique relics from the Grand Treasure Hunt (秘宝大作战), all worth their Level 5 slot: Lucky Statue (幸运神像,
  dungeon double-reward chance 10%), Primal Gem (原初宝石, gem acquisition 10%), Golden Sacred Tree (黄金神木, home
  wood output +10%, our translation), Lucky Cat Statue (招财猫神像, home Rolla output +10%, our translation), a
  pick-one Mythic relic (crit or block), and from S4 the Hourglass (Chrono Sand output) [S-90][S-92][S-96].
- The last two Treasure Hunt rounds of a season are poor value: skip them and carry the Aurodrasil Energy (世界树
  能量) into the next season's rounds [S-96].
- Chaos Invasion ranking pays Primal Forge (远古熔炉) shards, which raise wood output [S-92][S-97].
- Relic awakening (觉醒) of Rare and Epic relics is expensive and not worth it early [S-92].
- Displayed relics: 4 slots give +20% of their stats; damage classes display their element's affinity [S-92].

## Gear, affixes and gems after Loong Haven

- **Base affixes decide an account from Season 3 on.** The "four basic affixes" are double crit (crit rate + crit
  damage), crit + accuracy, block + crit resistance, and double block; they triple on entering a new season, while
  ordinary affixes at most double. 10 affix slots, each basic affix scored 3 to 5 [S-92].
- Recommended sets: Sorcerer 5 double crit + 5 crit-accuracy; Sage 5 crit-accuracy + 5 block-crit-resistance;
  Duelist 4 crit-accuracy + 6 block-crit-resistance; Knight 10 double block; healer 5 healing [S-92].
- Special affixes: "auto-protect" (自动保护: strong in PvE, nerfed), "proficiency" (娴熟: best for casters, weapon
  only, very rare), freeze (out of print) [S-92].
- Inheriting a second affix costs about 1,200 refinement stones per piece; a single affix about 300 per piece, and
  a cross-season inheritance includes one free re-roll [S-92]. Season 1 gear cannot be inherited; from Season 2 it
  can [S-93].
- Do not inherit affixes into the first (pre-season) dungeon's gear; wait for the second dungeon, whose gear gets
  the season bonus [S-96].
- Once you can pass a dungeon's threshold, stop crafting gear and sell the shards; later seasons raise thresholds
  anyway [S-93].
- Gems: levels 1-8 are cheap, level 9 turns expensive; cap 13 at Ignis; sockets 2 / 3 / 4 at average enhancement
  130 / 160 / 190 [S-92][S-97][S-91].
- Season rating (3S gear) in S3: two Divine and three Mythic pieces from the last non-Purgatory dungeon, later
  relaxed [S-92].

## Long-term F2P advice from CN veterans

What they say to do:

1. Buy Material Realm tools every day from day 1 (2 of each, "2222"); skipping it costs the whole season's rating [S-92].
2. Keep 1,000 Dawnium in reserve for the Mysterious Merchant (a Mythic Fantomon offer costs about 5,000) [S-92].
3. Dawnium order: reserve, Material Realm tools, 2 extra dungeon runs, ingredients for Epic and Mythic dishes,
   skill pulls until the class-change requirement, Fantomon pulls; never relic pulls [S-92].
4. Hoard Material Realm materials and use them at the season's top rank, when each swing yields most; if the
   season target is out of reach, carry them over [S-92][S-96].
5. Before a new season (from 7 days out): keep stamina at 300 and let the overflow go to recovery (cap 1200);
   stock Rolla above all (the usual shortfall: 160 to 190 on five pieces costs about 7.5M ore and 15M Rolla; relic
   resonance 16 to 19 about 6.45M Chrono Sand and 11.6M Rolla), friendship points, Destiny Fruit, map-opening
   tools (drills, water mines, blocks, keys) and big-mine attempts [S-96].
6. In a new kingdom: do not spend all Temporal Crystals (时间水晶) at once (open the next goddess statue first for
   a higher bonus); bank 36 hours of bed EXP; delay gathering and the weekly 50 big-mine digs until the middle
   rank if the week allows [S-96].
7. Buy map-opening tools first in every kingdom shop; the feather kingdom needs tools from the mountain and marsh
   kingdoms [S-92].
8. Companions: all to 20, crit and block companions to 50, all to 70, then crit and block ones to 100 [S-92].
9. Dishes: eat each Epic+ dish 10 times (the bonus drops after 10) [S-92].
10. Sell Divine shards and gear on the auction house for Dawnium; zero spenders should favour PvE over PvP [S-92].

What they call traps or regret:

- Spending vouchers before tier 3, or on non-core skills [S-92].
- Pulling relics with Dawnium; chasing gear re-rolls as a zero spender [S-92].
- Using Destiny Fruit and friendship points before the new kingdom opens, or materials at a low rank [S-96].
- Arriving at a new season with ore and sand but no Rolla [S-96].
- Inheriting good affixes into pre-season gear [S-96].
- Buying Golden Beetle, Shapeshifting Mirror or Rename Card in the Dawnium shop [S-92].
- Refreshing Wonder Dispatch as a free player [S-92].
- Chasing gem level 9+ early; awakening Rare and Epic relics early [S-92].
- Going full-time support: even spenders lose progress that way [S-92].
- Holding back for its own sake: the plan is "pull to the change level each tier", not "never pull" [S-92].

Season targets a zero spender set in CN Season 1: 128 and 152 Primostar tiers (character Lv 127, skills 125,
Fantomons 125, gear 115, relic +12); gear level is the one uncapped score, so missing points are bought with ore
[S-92]. One zero spender's ratings: Season 1 overall A (joined late), Season 2 overall SS with about 21.5M CP and
Astral Pact level 630 [S-92].

## Cart, home, economy and reworks

- Cart: stone and wood first to Lv 40, then stone and ore 10 levels ahead of wood, wood 5 ahead of Chrono Sand,
  the rest behind; stone and ore cap at 72 in Season 1 [S-92]. A quick multi-level upgrade button was added in
  2026-04 [S-97].
- Home shop refresh costs in Loong Haven: 4K, 8K, 12K, 20K, 30K, 48K, 80K; clearing the shop costs 100-150K Rolla
  there and 250K+ in the feather kingdom, where only about 3 refreshes are affordable at first [S-92].
- Reworks and resets seen in CN: eleven skills rolled back with refunds (2026-04-29); Knight shield skill nerfed
  (2025-09-09); auto-protect affix nerfed; Sage Hexed Blast nerfed; dungeon thresholds raised after release in S4;
  season-rating gear standard lowered in S3; guild farm changed to a tile grid; home visiting added; a returner
  buff added. No system was wiped: the season reset stays "levels above the cap" only [S-92][S-95][S-96][S-97].
- CN's season model has no catch-up: skipping a season leaves you behind for good, which is why a catch-up system
  was promised for the half-year version [S-92][S-90].
