# Relics

<!-- index: Read before drawing the Aeonic Gacha, spending Chrono Sand or Destiny Fruits, or choosing which relics to display -->

> **Summary:** Relics are collected per kingdom and per element; owning and ascending them is permanent power,
> and Relic Resonance (levelling the four displayed relics of an element with Chrono Sand) is the biggest
> power source early in every season. Level all five elements, your class's element first; aim for about 70%
> completion of each kingdom's relic pool before moving on; display unique relics (Lucky Statue, Primal Gem)
> and special-stat relics first [S-01].
> **Applies:** all seasons (Loong Haven pool details: Season 2)
> **Related:** [Gacha](gacha.md), [Currencies](currencies.md), [Material Realms](material-realms.md),
> [Stamina](stamina.md), [Exploration](exploration.md), [Goddess statues](goddess-statues.md),
> [Events](events.md), [Seasons](seasons.md), [Astral Pact](astral-pact.md),
> data: [relics.json](../data/relics.json), rules R-105, R-106, R-159, R-166, R-167, R-172

## The system in one picture

```
Auroral Badges ----> Aeonic Gacha ----\
Destiny Fruits ----> Destiny Exploration --> relics + relic shards --> Activate (Workbench)
Events / shops ----> unique relics ----/                               |-> Ascend (stars, 20 own shards)   permanent
                                                                       |-> Display 4 per element
Chrono Sand + Rolla -------------------------------------------------> Enhance displayed relics -> Resonance Level
                                                                         (permanent up to +10, above that seasonal)
```

- Relics are a progression layer of their own; they are not worn in gear slots [S-25]. They live at the
  **Workbench** in the house (R-166, R-172) and are drawn at the **Aeonic Gacha** stone at Home (R-167).
- Size of the system: about 855 relic pieces across 6 regions [S-25]. The Relic Gallery has tabs
  Verdantglade, Cinder Ridge, Aqualis, Loong Haven and Other [S-22] ❓.

## Elements, Affinity and Aegis

- Five elements: **Fire, Water, Wind, Light, Dark** [S-01, S-25]. The Workbench has one tab per element (R-166).
- Each relic carries an elemental **Affinity** (damage side) or **Aegis** (resist side) line; the Workbench
  shows Affinity and Aegis counters per element (R-166) [S-25]. Example: Lucky Statue has Light Affinity 200
  [S-01, page image].
- Total elemental Affinity also opens **Gates of Elements** on the map (R-159).
- "Relics come in elements of 4": four display slots per element (R-105, R-166).

## Rarities

- Relic rarities seen: Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic [S-02, S-25], plus a Divine event relic [S-03].
  The gacha has one tier per rarity (Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic) and an Elemental tier ([Gacha](gacha.md)).
- Community colour talk: blue = Rare, purple = Epic, orange/gold = Legendary or Mythic [S-04, S-24] ❓.
- A normal F2P account ends a kingdom with all or most Rare and Epic relics; Mythic and Legendary relics are
  what paying players fill in [S-24] ❓.

## Activate, Enhance, Ascend, Replace

| Action | What it does | Cost | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Activate** | A newly drawn relic shows under its rarity with "Activate"; activating adds its Aegis/Affinity and stats and places it on a display slot if one is free | Free | R-172 |
| **Enhance** | Raises a displayed relic's level (+1, +2, ...) | Chrono Sand and Rolla: 1K sand / 1.8K Rolla for +1 -> +2 and +2 -> +3; 2K / 3.6K for +3 -> +4 | R-172 |
| **Ascend** | Raises the relic's stars; grows its Affinity/Aegis line; permanent | 20 of the relic's own shards (first star observed); up to 6 stars ❓ | R-172; [S-25], [S-03] |
| **Replace** | Swaps which relic sits in a display slot | Free ❓; the bot must not press it (R-172) | R-172 |

- Levelling a relic grows one main stat (most relics: SPD, ATK, DEF or HP; a minority: DMG RES, DMG Boost,
  Crit Rate, Crit RES); starring grows the elemental line [S-25].
- About 75 relics have an extra bonus that grows by completing a task, many tied to a gear slot [S-25] ❓.
- Some relic groups form **sets** with bonuses at 2, 3 and 4 owned pieces (example: Cinder Ridge "Faint Ember
  Glow": Quake Pick, Pyrecrown, Ignic Loop, Blade Saw) [S-22] ❓. QY Maple's pool table also has "Complete
  Set" and "Epic Set" rows [S-02, page image].

## Relic Resonance

- Each element displays 4 relics. The element's **Resonance Level** is set by those four: "the elemental stats
  and effects of Relics on display increase by 20%" (Workbench text, R-166; same line in [S-01] screenshot at
  Resonance Level 10).
- Raising all four displayed relics to the same level raises the Resonance Level, and every other relic of
  that element is counted at that level [S-24] ❓. This is why relics are enhanced "in bands", four at a time
  (R-105).
- SOF: Relic Resonance is "one of your most important sources of both seasonal and permanent power"; do not
  pick one element and ignore the others, **all five** must be progressed [S-01].

### Soft cap per season

| Season | Resonance soft cap | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Season 1 | +10 | [S-01] |
| Season 2 | +13 | [S-01] |
| Later | Rises with the seasonal level cap | [S-01] |

- Levels above the cap reset at season end [S-01]; Fandom states it as "Relic level above +10" is seasonal and
  resets [S-03]. This page reads the two together as: +10 is kept forever; anything above it lasts for the
  season ❓ (whether the kept level rises in later seasons is not stated).

### What to display (resonance stat priority)

Displayed relics get the +20%, so choose the four per element in this order [S-01]:

1. **Unique relics** (Lucky Statue, Primal Gem)
2. **Special stats** (Crit Rate, Block %, ... whichever your class uses)
3. **Fixed values** (e.g. ATK +100)
4. **% values** (e.g. ATK +10%)

SOF's Light example at Resonance Level 10: Flat ATK, Lucky Statue (dungeon bonus drop), Flat ATK, Crit DMG
[S-01, page image]. "Always have the Lucky Statue in your main relic line for the 20% resonance bonus": its
10% effect shows as +2% on display [S-01].

### Which element first (per class)

Max your class's preferred element first, then the rest; eventually all are levelled [S-01].

| Class | Element | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Duelist | Fire | |
| Sage | Dark | |
| Knight | Water or Light | Water for AoE; Light otherwise |
| Sorcerer | Wind, Water, Fire or Light (your preference) | Wind: PvP. Water: better in 4v4 than PvE. Fire: better in PvE than PvP. Light: equally good in both |

Source: [S-01]. For a Mage -> Sorcerer account that mainly does PvE, that means Fire or Light first; a Sage
goes Dark first.

Our rule R-105 orders elements by the character's highest affinity and keeps all elements within one band of
each other; SOF says to max the preferred element first. See [conflicts](../conflicts.md).

## Chrono Sand

- Chrono Sand pays for Enhance (with Rolla) (R-172, R-106). Sources: the Sand Material Realm, overworld sand
  nodes at 5 stamina each (R-106), the Cart's Chrono Sand row (R-020), Grand Sand Mine spawns, gift codes.
- Timing [S-01, S-02]: **start of a season -> stamina and extra Material Realm tools go to Sand**, because
  Relic Resonance gives the most seasonal power early; **mid-season -> switch to Ore**, as gear catches up.
- Daily realm split recommended for F2P: 4 Ore / 2 Rolla / 4 Battle Essence / 4 Sand ("4244"), or "4444"
  [S-01]. See [Material Realms](material-realms.md).

## Kingdom relic pools and the 70% target

- Every kingdom has its own relic pool and gacha banner (Verdantglade's banner is "Sylvan Relics", R-167).
- **Target: about 70% overall completion per region, with as many Rare/Epic relics as possible, before moving
  on** [S-01]. Same target for Loong Haven, "especially in the first pool, since you can get relics with
  destiny fruit" [S-02]. EOG gives 50-70% and says F2P accounts typically end near 70%, paying accounts near
  90% [S-20, S-24] ❓.
- Why early pulls matter: a new pool is empty, so each pull is far more likely to be a new relic than in a
  saturated pool [S-02].
- Each kingdom's Mythic/Legendary relics have different special-stat focuses; this only matters for late
  optimisation [S-01].

## Destiny Fruits

- Using a Destiny Fruit during exploration ("Destiny Exploration") unearths relics hidden in that region and
  may summon Molly or the Mysterious Merchant to Home with discounted goods ("some deals may not be what they
  seem") [S-03].
- Check the Relic Set for missing pieces and their exact locations; the nearest Goddess Statue card lists the
  region's relics [S-03] (our statue cards show "Regional Relics", R-070).
- Destiny Fruits lie on the map as pickups (R-161) and come from codes and events ❓ [S-04].
- The World tab's Explore page has "Destiny Exploration Goals" up to 100 uses (R-116 claims them).
- Use: fill the missing Rare/Epic pieces of the current kingdom once gacha pulls mostly give duplicates
  [S-22] ❓. Tier D advice for F2P: prefer new relics (collection) over shard top-ups [S-04] ❓.

## Permanent vs seasonal

| Part | Permanent? | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Owning/activating relics (collection, Affinity/Aegis) | Permanent | [S-01] |
| Relic ascensions (stars) | Permanent | [S-03] |
| Relic level / Resonance up to +10 | Permanent | [S-03], [S-01] |
| Relic level above the cap (+11 to +13 in Season 2) | Seasonal: reset at season end | [S-01], [S-03] |
| "Season relic level" | Rated by the Astral Pact at season end, converting seasonal effort into permanent Primostars | [S-01] |

Relics can still be pulled in later seasons, which is why SOF ranks the once-per-season Astral Pact above
them for end-of-season decisions [S-01].

## What the bot does today

- Draw x10 on any tier whenever 100 badges or tickets are available, never with Dawnium, never x1 (R-167).
- Activate new relics, Enhance while affordable, never Replace (R-172); enhance in bands (R-105).
- Guide advice that goes beyond these rules (spending Dawnium on the relic gacha, choosing display relics) is
  listed in [conflicts](../conflicts.md) for mae to decide.
