# Events

<!-- index: Read when an event is running or about to start: what it is, when it comes back, its currency, and the best free-to-play choice -->

> **Summary:** Three recurring events decide long-term power: the Grand Treasure Hunt (every Saturday; save one week, buy the Lucky Statue or Primal Gem relic the next), Chaos Invasion (class damage ranking; afterwards buy only the Primal Forge from the Mysterious Merchant) and the Wheel of Destiny (pick Skill Shard Vouchers first). Everything else is sign-ins and free tracks: claim daily, never pay.
> **Applies:** all seasons

## Grand Treasure Hunt

### What it is

- A weekly event shop held every Saturday in the SOF calendars [S-01] [S-02]. You spend an event currency through five reward levels; Level 5 holds the week's unique item [S-43].
- The unique Level 5 item alternates. On "MUST BUY" weeks it is one of two special relics; on "SAVE" weeks it is a lesser item, and you keep your currency so that next week you can afford Level 5 [S-01] [S-02].
- The currency carries over from week to week (that is the whole strategy) [S-01]. EOG calls it Aurodrasil Energy and says about 3,240 is needed to reach Level 5, with roughly 4,440 available every two weeks ❓ [S-43].

### The two relics worth saving for

| Relic | Effect | Why it matters | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lucky Statue | "Dungeon: 2x Rating Reward Rate 10%" at Shard 0 (Light Affinity 200); grows with shards, up to 60% ❓ | Doubles dungeon shard and gear rewards for the rest of the game; put it in the main relic line for the 20% resonance bonus | [S-01] [S-43] |
| Primal Gem | Gem acquisition boost, 10% up to 60% ❓ | More gems from every source | [S-01] [S-43] |

- Both are "unique relics", the top of the relic resonance priority: Unique relic > special stats > fixed value > % value [S-01]. See [Relics](relics.md).
- Each repeat purchase adds a shard to the relic, so the "MUST BUY" continues every time it returns [S-02].

## Chaos Invasion

### What it is

- A damage-ranking event against a Chaos boss ("Chaos Tyrant" [S-42]). Rankings are **per class**: Duelists against Duelists, Sorcerers against Sorcerers, Knights against Knights, Sages against Sages [S-03].
- Unlocks at Lv 50 and starts after a 7-day countdown [S-42].
- Flow: phase 1, collect Chaos Shards on the map (cap 100; small nodes give 5-20, medium 25-50, large 50-100); phase 2, with 100 shards the Chaos Rifts open and you challenge the Chaos Tyrant. The first challenge pays a fixed amount of Chaos Crystals [S-42].
- It can be done solo; companions are not allowed [S-03].

### Boss mechanics and party setup

- The boss has very low armor, so damage numbers are far higher than in dungeons. That favors burst damage and stacked team buffs [S-01] [S-03].
- Enter as a party of 4: **1 damage dealer and 3 supports**. Run 4 rounds, rotating so each player takes one turn as the damage dealer. This applies to every class, tanks and healers included, because every class has team-buff skills [S-01] [S-03].
- Solo damage numbers mislead: team buffs behave differently (e.g. Duelist Flame Aura interactions), so judge a build by party runs. Solo is fine for quick tests or a last run before logging off [S-03].
- Most players are DPS, so a perfect rotation group is hard to form; it helps the rank but is not required [S-01].

### Ranking rewards

| Rank | Reward | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30 Primal Forge Shards, 20 Covenite, 20 Legendary Auroral Badges, 200 Chaos Crystals | [S-42] |
| 501+ | 8 Covenite, 100 Chaos Crystals | [S-42] |
| between | ❓ | |

- The reward that matters is the **Primal Forge Shard**, not Covenite or badges [S-03].
- Realistic targets: rank 20-50 is fine for regular players (top 10-20 later as the server thins); only paying players should chase top 5. Do not over-invest for a few places [S-03].

### Mysterious Merchant and the Primal Forge

- The day after the event ends, a Mysterious Merchant (Mysterious NPC) appears in your Home and sells for Chaos Crystals [S-01] [S-03].
- **Buy only the Primal Forge.** Keep buying it each event until it is maxed (up to Immortal rarity) [S-01] [S-03].
- If you cannot afford it, dismiss the merchant ("kick him out") and keep the crystals for the next Chaos Invasion [S-01] [S-03].
- What it does: the Primal Forge raises the Cart's Raw Ore output. More ore means higher gear levels, which weigh most in the end-of-season rating [S-03]. See [Cart and home](cart-and-home.md) and [Astral Pact](astral-pact.md).
- ❓ Primal Forge prices per level and the rest of the merchant's stock are not listed anywhere we could read.

## Void Rift

- The Journal's Weekly Events card on our account shows Void Rift [R-094]. Unlocks at Lv 50 [S-42].
- Rift markers appear on every region's map for a 24-hour window, every other Sunday, alternating with Chaos Invasion ❓ [S-43].
- Team of up to 4; up to 5 floors per rift; 10 reward attempts per event, which do not carry over [S-42] [S-43].
- After each cleared floor a roll decides whether the rift goes on, so floor 5 is a bonus; aim for 3 to 4 floors ❓ [S-43].
- Floors 1-2 are mob packs, floors 3-4 an elite with adds; healing happens only between floors ❓ [S-43].
- Rewards: relic pulls whose rarity is rolled per pull (up to Mythic on higher floors) and currency scaled by floors cleared ❓ [S-43].
- A rift someone else is inside cannot be entered: go to the next marker. The region does not change rewards ❓ [S-43].
- Suggested group: 1 healer, 1 tank, 2 DPS [S-43]. A Sage is the natural healer seat; see [Sage](../classes/sage.md).

## Wheel of Destiny

- A prize wheel spun with Lucky Tickets [R-147]. Lucky Tickets come from the Guild Shop (1.5K Guild Tokens, limit 1 per week) [R-114], Vendor Booth point milestones and other shops; the wheel itself can award extra tickets [S-01].
- Lucky Wheel structures on the map open the same screen; the first spin on each map wheel is free while its zone is below 100% explored, and it counts toward the zone's exploration [R-147].
- Reward priority [S-01]:

| Priority | Take |
|---|---|
| 1 | Skill Shard Vouchers |
| 2 | Gateway Keys, Auroral Elemental Badges |
| 3 | Legendary Auroral Badges, Covenite, Bond Trinkets |
| 4 | Legendary Fantomon resources (wool, egg, essence), 2888 Dawnium, additional Lucky Tickets |

- Skill Vouchers are among the most valuable items in the game because they max skills that stay useful across class tiers [S-01]. See [Skills](skills.md).
- Gateway Keys open blocked map areas with extra Lost Goddess Statues [S-01]. See [Goddess statues](goddess-statues.md).
- The bot spins only the free first spin of a map wheel; paid spins wait for mae's decision [R-147].
- sxswiki has two Wheel of Destiny pages; they add nothing reliable beyond the list above [S-04].

## Pilgrimage (battle pass)

- The battle pass currency is the Pilgrimage Point, earned by normal play (daily activity) ❓ [S-46]. The Mall sells a "Pilgrimage Pass" for real money [R-121].
- A pass has 70 tiers, each with a free lane reward and a premium lane reward; skipping a tier costs 100 Dawnium ❓ [S-46].
- There are permanent "Normal" passes and rotating "Season" passes; headline rewards are a Legendary relic and, on Season passes, a self-selected Legendary Fantomon (lane not stated) ❓ [S-46].
- Our plan: free lane only. Claim free rewards; never buy the pass (real money) and do not skip tiers with Dawnium, since daily play fills the track.

Pattern for any new seasonal event (from Journey Overture) [S-03]:
1. A 14-day sign-in with a cosmetic at the end: claim daily.
2. A stage or quest track that pays an event currency: do the free quests.

4. Outfit sales and packs: real money, skip.
