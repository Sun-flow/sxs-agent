# Stamina

<!-- index: Read when deciding what to spend stamina on, how much to keep back, or how to bank it before a new season -->

> **Summary:** Stamina refills 5 an hour up to 300 (120 a day) and anything above is lost, so never sit at the cap. Spend it first on commissions that need it, then on pickups of the season's priority material: sand at the start of a season, ore from the middle. Claim the free 30 in the Vault daily. Bank about 300 before a new kingdom opens.
> **Applies:** all seasons
> **Related:** [Commissions](commissions.md), [Material Realms](material-realms.md), [Currencies](currencies.md), [Seasons](seasons.md), [Kingdom rank](kingdom-rank.md), rules R-106, R-107, R-108, R-109, R-120, R-122

| Use | Cost | Gives | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resource Hunt pickups (Journal > Map Exploration) | 5 each | Rolla, ore, sand or Battle Essence; an ore rock gave about +178 Raw Ore | [R-106][R-108] |

Free of stamina: special nodes with a green timer circle such as a Grand Mine (one free collection) [R-109];

## Best uses

1. **Commissions first.** SOF completes commissions aggressively "even when completing them requires stamina"
   [S-01]. Our rule keeps at least 70 stamina for the day's commissions [R-107].
2. **Then the season's priority material** [S-01][S-02]:

| Season phase | Spend stamina on | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Chrono Sand | Relic Resonance gives the most seasonal power early |
| Middle | Ore | gear catches up with relics |
| Last weeks | Ore | gear level weighs most in the Primostar rating |

3. **Rolla** only when out of it for upgrades; the "Rolla strategy" spends stamina on Rolla to fund shop refreshes,
   which SOF does not favour [S-01]. One site's opposite advice (Rolla nodes first) is tier D ❓ [S-39].

The same sand-then-ore order applies to extra Material Realm tools [S-01]. Our gather policy currently targets ore
and is due to switch to sand [R-107].

## Do not waste it

- Passive regeneration stops at 300, so spend before it fills [R-107]. From empty that takes 60 hours; with a
  daily session, spending down to the commission reserve each day never overflows.
- Stamina from items can sit above 300 (up to 1,200) [R-107], which is how a bank is built.

## Banking before a season

- "If possible, try to bank up 300 stamina before entering Loong Haven" [S-02]: enter the new kingdom with a full
  bar for its commissions and its new, richer pickups.
- One site adds: in the last days spend only enough for daily missions and hold the rest until the first Kingdom
  Rank step (Champion I) of the new season, because rank raises stamina rewards ❓ [S-34]. Material Realm rewards do
  scale with rank [S-03]; whether pickups do is unconfirmed ❓.
