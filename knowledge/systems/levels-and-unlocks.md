# Levels and unlocks

<!-- index: Read to know what opens at which character level, where the level caps are, and where EXP comes from -->

> **Summary:** Character level comes mostly from passive bed EXP, so it is driven by statues and time, and it is
> capped by Kingdom Rank. Levels up to 100 are permanent; levels above 100 are seasonal and reset to 100. The table
> below lists every known feature unlock by level; the big Season 2 ones are T4 class 106, Adult Fantomon 108,
> Demonbind Tower 116, top rank 120, second dungeon 130.
> **Applies:** all seasons
> **Related:** [Kingdom Rank](kingdom-rank.md), [Goddess Statues](goddess-statues.md), [Seasons](seasons.md),
> [World and kingdoms](world-and-kingdoms.md), [Classes overview](classes-overview.md), data in
> [unlocks.json](../data/unlocks.json); rules R-011, R-101, R-118

- A level cap stops EXP until the rank advances; banked bed EXP is applied on rank-up [R-118].
- After Lv 100 each level costs about 50,000 EXP more than the one before (a calculator's default assumption) [S-51].
- "Below level 100 is permanent, above level 100 is seasonal" is the simplest way to read every system [S-03].
- Skills are capped by character level [R-037].

## Where EXP comes from

| Source | How | Notes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bed (AFK EXP) | Passive, stored up to 36 hours | The main source; rate set by Blessing Level and Lost Goddess Statues' Home EXP % | [R-101][R-070][S-03] |
| Goddess Statues / Lost Goddess Statues | Raise the bed rate | [Goddess Statues](goddess-statues.md) | [S-03] |
| Free daily bed speed-up | Once a day | Use after the day's statues are lit | [R-035][S-03] |
| Temporal Crystals | 2 hours of bed and Cart output each | Same timing rule | [S-03][S-02] |
| Quests, exploration, fights | Small, early | The story line gives the first levels | [R-044] |
| Astral Pact "Exp Gain +2%" nodes | Permanent bonus | [Astral Pact](astral-pact.md) | [S-02][S-51] |
| Hero's Guide relic | Boosts EXP gain; from the Stellaris Path ❓ | Single-source | [S-52] |
| Monthly Pass | Boosts home production | Real money: not used | [S-03] |

Before a season starts, bed EXP is left uncollected for 36 hours so it lands in the new season [S-02].

are passed again every season from Season 2 on ❓ (T4 class itself stays).
