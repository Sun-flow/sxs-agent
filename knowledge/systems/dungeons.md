# Dungeons

<!-- index: Read before entering any dungeon, when planning CP targets for the next dungeon unlock, or when building the daily and weekly content routine -->

> **Summary:** The Daily Dungeon is the 4-player gear dungeon of each kingdom and the main source of gear and gear shards. A new one opens on a fixed server day; each difficulty is gated by a hard CP threshold (and sometimes a level). Reach the threshold of the newest dungeon on its opening day, bank attempts (up to 6) beforehand, wear the region's "+dungeon reward" gear and the Lucky Statue relic, and ask for a carry on the highest difficulty you can enter.
> **Applies:** all seasons (numbers given for Season 1 and Season 2)
> **Related:** [Gear](gear.md), [Relics](relics.md), [Seasons](seasons.md), [Astral Pact](astral-pact.md), [Material Realms](material-realms.md), [Towers](towers.md), [Monsters](monsters.md), [Events](events.md), [Auction house](auction-house.md), [Kingdom rank](kingdom-rank.md), [Levels and unlocks](levels-and-unlocks.md), rules R-133, R-040, R-071, R-091, R-082, R-086, R-015

## Daily Dungeons (team gear dungeons)

### How a run works

- A Daily Dungeon is built for 4 players. The Team panel fills empty seats with NPC companions (Akane, Qin, Killian) or you invite players; entering is free [R-133]. There is also matchmaking [S-42].
- Inside is a room map: combat rooms, buff rooms, healing fountains and one boss room at the end [S-43]. Our first run showed the cards "1 More Floor(s)", "Dungeon Room Map" and "Highest tier" [R-133].
- The run ends with a rating from D to S, a chest per player and a Claim Rewards button [R-133]. The rating decides how many shards you get (table below) [S-42].
- The score depends on how far you get, not on which rooms you pick, so route straight to the boss ❓ [S-43].
- Fights are automatic like every battle [R-001]. Heal between rooms; a defeat ends the run [R-091].
- Players have up to seven skill loadout slots, so keep an AoE loadout for rooms and a survival loadout for hard bosses ❓ [S-43] [S-49].

### Attempts, the bank ("rest bonus") and paid refreshes

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Free attempts | 2 per day | [S-42] [S-43] |
| Paid attempts | 2 more per day: the 3rd run costs 100 Dawnium, the 4th 150 Dawnium | [S-43] [S-45] |
| More than 4 runs a day | Only through paid cards and packs (not for us) | [S-43] |
| Gear-claim chances | 2 per day, more with spending | [S-42] |
| Bank / rest bonus | Unused attempts stack; the stack holds at most 6 attempts and overflow is lost | [S-02] [S-43] |
| Daily reset | 8:00 server time (UTC-5) | [R-024] |

- The SOF guide calls the stack the "dungeon rest bonus" and says to arrive at a new dungeon with it stacked for 6 attempts [S-02]. EOG says the same thing as "banked runs cap at 6" and adds: stop claiming dungeon rewards one day before a new season, which banks 2 extra attempts [S-43].
- ❓ Whether the rest bonus is literally stored attempts or a reward bonus applied to the next 6 runs is not confirmed by our own play. Either way the action is the same: skip the old dungeon for two days before a new dungeon opens, then run 6 times on day one.
- If you buy only one paid attempt in the whole game per day, the gear dungeon is the best place for it, because gear shards have no other source ❓ [S-43].

### Drops: gear, shards and rating

- Dungeon gear has the level of its dungeon and is the biggest source of seasonal CP: a full Divine set from the last Season 1 dungeon is worth almost 3M CP [S-03].
- Gear drops as whole pieces or as **shards**; shards combine into a piece. A Mythic piece needs 100 shards, and dismantling a Mythic piece returns 20 shards [S-42].
- Mythic gear does not exist before Cinder Ridge [S-42].
- Shards per run by rating (after Cinder Ridge): B = 6, A = 7, S = 8 Mythic shards; Hard with an S rating gives 12. With up to 4 runs a day a Mythic item takes about 3 days [S-42].
- Auroradrasil's first clear (rating B) dropped 96 Raw Ore, Beastclaw Boots and Beasthide Armor, drop level 18 to 30 [R-133].
- Shards and gear of the **current** dungeon can be sold to other players for Dawnium ([Auction house](auction-house.md)). When the next dungeon opens, old shards lose their sale value and can only be dismantled, so sell them before that (within a day of the unlock notice) [S-43] [S-02].
- Late in a season, Aquaorigin Divine shards and gear become cheap; buy them if you need gear rating for the [Astral Pact](astral-pact.md) [S-02].
- Rating target for the end of Season 1: 2 Divine + 3 Mythic pieces from Hellhold give an SSS gear rating; 2 Divine Hellhold + 2 Divine Aquaorigin + 1 Mythic Hellhold also reaches SSS [S-02].

### First dungeon of a season (pre-season)

- Loong Haven opens with a **pre-season**: its first dungeon (Demonseal Gorge) does not count toward seasonal progression milestones, and its gear is not "seasonal gear" [S-02].
- Affixes rolled on first-dungeon gear are at their weakest value; gear from every later dungeon of the season gets a bonus (200% on unique affix lines, 20% on standard lines). Affixes can be moved from first-dungeon gear to later gear, and the bonus then applies [S-02]. See [Gear](gear.md).
- Two valid plans for the first dungeon: sell the shards and gear for sure Dawnium (the Cart soon drops Legendary gear stronger than this dungeon's Mythic), or keep rolling for a top affix to carry forward. Either way, making the next dungeon's CP threshold comes first [S-02].
- The Cart helps on opening days: a high-level Cart can drop Legendary gear of the new tier at once (the author got a piece 15K CP above his Mythic on the Aquaorigin opening) [S-01]. See [Cart and home](cart-and-home.md).

## The carry strategy and "+dungeon reward" gear

The fastest gearing loop in the game, from the SOF guide [S-01]:

1. Meet the CP threshold of the newest dungeon difficulty you can reach. That is the only thing you must do yourself.
2. Before entering, equip gear pieces whose extra stat boosts dungeon rewards, as long as you still meet the threshold with them on.
3. Enter the highest difficulty you can and ask a strong player for a carry.
4. Better and doubled drops raise CP, which opens the next difficulty; repeat.
5. When you are fully geared, keep doing it and sell the extra shards for Dawnium.

| Stat (as shown in game) | Effect | Seen on | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Aqualis Dungeons - 2x Rating Reward Rate: +5%" | Chance to double the rating reward (shards and gear) | Legendary boots (Ocean Greaves) | [S-01] |
| "Aqualis Dungeons - Gear Drop Quality Ascension Rate +10%" | Chance that a gear drop comes one rarity higher (Legendary becomes Mythic) | Epic boots (Tidechaser's Leggings) | [S-01] |
| Lucky Statue relic: "Dungeon: 2x Rating Reward Rate 10%" | Same doubling chance, from a relic; 10% at Shard 0, +2% in the pictured resonance, up to 60% with ascensions ❓ | Grand Treasure Hunt relic | [S-01] [S-43] |

- These gear stats are **region specific**: an "Aqualis Dungeons" stat works only in Aqualis dungeons. Keep one set per kingdom and wear the right one [S-01].
- Keep such pieces even when they are low rarity; they are swap-in gear, not your fighting set [S-01].
- Always place the Lucky Statue in your main relic line for the 20% resonance bonus [S-01]. It is bought from the [Grand Treasure Hunt](events.md). See [Relics](relics.md).
- A carry cannot rank for you or build your account: spend your own resources on what nobody can carry (permanent power, your threshold) [S-01].
- Team-up is allowed for our bot [R-057]; the Team panel has World Call, Invite and Nearby [R-077]. See [Monsters](monsters.md) for team-up mechanics.

## Map mini-dungeons

- A mini-dungeon is any map card named Ruins, Cave, Tower, Dungeon, Pit, Mine, Lair, Den or Fortress, or any card showing "First Clear Rewards" [R-071].
- Temple Ruins (Originisle III): several floors, 6 enemies per floor, a floor card with Next Floor; the floor count and summit reward are unknown ❓ [R-040].
- Mines such as Ironvein Pit (Verdantglade VII): First Clear Rewards (Refined Ore 100, Raw Ore 640), battles up to 25 rounds [R-071].
- Heal on the floor card between floors; a lost fight resets the dungeon [R-091].
- Mini-dungeons are part of an area's 100% exploration, which is what unlocks its Goddess Statue [R-065]. See [Exploration](exploration.md) and [Goddess statues](goddess-statues.md).
