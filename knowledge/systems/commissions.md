# Commissions

<!-- index: Read when choosing which Daily Commission to take or which reward to pick, and how much stamina to keep for them -->

> **Summary:** Do commissions every day, even the ones that cost stamina. When a choice is offered, take Premium Commissions first (they can pay companion journals and sellable recipes), then whatever pays the kingdom currency you need for the next kingdom tool.
> **Applies:** all seasons
> **Related:** [Stamina](stamina.md), [Companions](companions.md), [Shops](shops.md#kingdom-shops), [Currencies](currencies.md), [Exploration](exploration.md), rules R-107, R-128

Our own game has not mapped the commission screen yet, so this page rests on the SOF guide; details marked ❓ need a
first read in game.

## How they work

- Daily Commissions are small tasks handed out each day [S-01]. The guide's screenshot shows two cards side by
  side, each with a task, a companion portrait, a progress counter, three reward icons and a Go button [S-01]:

| Card in the screenshot | Task | Rewards shown |
|---|---|---|
| "Eliminate Dark Slimes" | kill a set number (counter shown, about 0/5 ❓) | a leaf icon x150 (Abundance Leaves), a coin icon 11.5K, a third item x6 ❓ |
| "Collect Vital Thornballs" (tagged "Combinable") | collect 0/5 | an item x150, an item x2, an item x5 ❓ |

- Tasks are of the kill and collect kind, done on the overworld, which is why some cost stamina [S-01].
- "Go" sends you to the task; "Combinable" is not explained ❓ (likely that it can be done together with another
  commission).
- Other names for the same system on guide sites: "Delegate Tasks" ❓ [S-35].
- Where the screen is, how many commissions there are a day and when they reset are not in our sources ❓; dailies
  in general reset at 8:00 server time (UTC-5) [R-024].

## Rewards and priority

| Priority | Take | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Premium Commissions | chance of recipes (to sell) and journals (to unlock companions) | [S-01] |
| 2 | Commissions paying kingdom currency | buys kingdom tools: about 3K leaves for a Gateway Key or Withering Potion in Verdantglade | [S-01] |
| 3 | Commissions paying Material Realm tools | Iron Hammers, Gold Pickaxes ❓ | [S-36] |
| 4 | The rest | Rolla and materials | [S-01] |

- "Since I know I need 3k leaves to buy a key/potion, I will aggressively push commissions rewarding leaves", and
  the same for other kingdoms' currencies depending on how close the next tool is [S-01].
- Commissions do not raise power directly; they pay for the tools that open areas with more Lost Goddess Statues
  [S-01]. See [Goddess statues](goddess-statues.md).

## Premium Commissions

- A better class of commission; SOF's first choice [S-01]. How one becomes available (a reward choice, a ticket, a
  random upgrade) is not stated ❓.
- Rewards: a chance of **recipes**, which can be sold on the player market, and **journals** [S-01]. Recipes for
  permanent food are known to come "occasionally" from daily commissions [S-31].

## Journals

- A journal is a gift item tied to one companion. Example: "Mysterious Merchant's Journal", type Gift, source
  Commission: "A gift to acquaint yourself with the Mysterious Merchant. Gift it after acquaintance to increase
  Affinity" [S-01].
- New companions are unlocked through journals from Premium Commissions [S-01]. Levelling companions gives a lot of
  stats, so journals are "very important" [S-01]. See [Companions](companions.md).

## Stamina cost

- Some commissions need stamina; do them anyway [S-01].
- Keep at least 70 stamina for the day's commissions before spending the rest on pickups [R-107].
- The stamina cost per commission is not recorded ❓.

## Related daily lists

Do not confuse commissions with the Daily Quest page (Menu > Quest: log in, 2 Vendor purchases, consume 30 stamina,
Material Realms x5, Fantasia Ascent, Conquest) [R-128], or with Guidance quests [R-099].
